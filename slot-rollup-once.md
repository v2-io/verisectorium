# Slot roll-up: seen in one kind

*2026-11-10. Slot names seen in exactly one segment kind (i = 1); the rest are in `slot-rollup.md`. A name here may be a real slot of that kind, or that kind's own content: nothing outside the kind can tell. Every slot name `segment-kinds.md` shows, one row per name, sorted by i, then c, then category. How a slot is carried, whether it is declared, and where it lives are collapsed. Names are reduced (markup, case, numbering, separators and trailing parentheticals dropped; the innermost part of a nested heading); names with a count under 3 are left out as noise (724 of them across both files), unless a store declares them or the ontology names them (182 kept). The mark after a name is the strongest it has in any kind: ` ➕` required and repeated in at least one kind; else ` 🞷` optional and repeated in at least one; else none, required and single in at least one; `﹖` only where it is optional in every kind. **c**: files or units carrying it, with stores that are copies of one another counted once. **i** (instances): how many segment kinds, store by store, carry it, copies again counted once; its spread. **category**: where the name places in the slot-intent ontology (`intents.py`) (names used in one store are placed there by keyword, which is looser), keyed in the Category key table after it. **segment-kinds**: the three kinds that carry it most, as `kind·store`, then how many more; store codes are keyed in the Store key table after it. **desc**: the purposes and notes the passes recorded (cut at 100 characters), leaving out what the name or its category already says; what a category is for is in the Category key. Generated by `msc/scratch/proto-instances/segment-kinds/intents.py` (gitignored).*
