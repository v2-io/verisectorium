# «verisectorium»
A place for cutting truth: a body of work where material brought in is dissected until each piece can contribute on its own, adjudicated and refined toward agreement with the truth it is held to, and reassembled into coherent results for others to comprehend and build on; worked on under high turnover; and true as stated, with its conditions, whenever stationary.

## Definition
A verisectorium takes in **influx**, material from outside its settled content, and **cuts** it until the pieces are independently contributory: each able to be right or wrong, decided or open, on its own. The pieces are **adjudicated** by minds and instruments whose acts are recorded. They are **refined** toward agreement with what the work is held to. And they are **reassembled** into coherent results that readers and downstream builders use and build on.

Concretely a verisectorium is: an **ontology** (the kinds of thing it may hold, the «dimensions|def/dimension» asked of them, and which of those are ‹determinant›s); a **corpus** of everything written in it; its **acta** of everything done to it; a **history**, the line of its «steady·states|def/steady-state»; and its **praxes**, what is expected of a mind working in it that no tool enforces. The word also names this repository and project, which describes verisectoria generally and builds the tooling (`v2`) for them; that sense is kept because the estate uses it, and it is not the referent of the relations above.

### Machinery and practical model
Two things are defined in this outline and must not be confused. The **machinery** is the meta-ontology the rest of the outline gives: eleven terms, under which content divides without end in principle and stops in practice at a few scales, the «veritoid», the «segment» and the projections that gather segments. The **practical model** is what a mind at work actually meets: an outline with marked rows, document-like segments rendered as markdown, an inbox beside each segment, a frontier to read, a steady state to reach, and the affordances each of those wants. The practical model is built on the machinery and is not the same thing as it; a tool may give an outline affordances a bare projection does not name without the machinery changing. Where the outline speaks of both, it says which. What the practical model cannot embed deterministically is written for the mind as a **praxis**: the engine enforces what it can, and the praxes say what is expected beyond that.

### Invariants
- At each of its steady states, no piece asserts more about itself than what has been done to it warrants, every reference holds, and whatever is still owed is owed by recorded acceptance with its reason.
- At least one of its projections is canonical, however experimental or nascent, and it is true as stated (for example, as initial untested hypotheses) at every steady state.
- A "provisional" segment of any kind is, to canon, only a claim that the suggestion for such a segment (etc.) exists.
- Every piece of its content is of a declared «kind».
- Nothing in its corpus is changed by hand, and nothing in its acta is typed; every change goes through a «changeset».

## Discussion
- **Why influx comes first in the definition.** Every verisectorium in the estate began the same way and keeps beginning that way: a pile of quotes pulled on a subject, spikes and generative drafts, empirical results, audits, an earlier theory's files, the notes a project was worked out in. The dissection is the thing itself, not a preliminary to it, and under turnover it is what lets the work compound instead of being redone: new results are built on adjudicated ones. In a theory the branching chain of claims is the engine of discovery; in a specification the accumulating decisions are.
- **Truth-centric.** What a verisectorium is for is agreement with the dimensions it declares as determinants: validity against logic, agreement with the world, fidelity to a source, consistency within a canon, and so on. Which, and against what, is each verisectorium's declaration, and the relation above requires at least one, because a verisectorium with no determinant has no sense of better. What it claims as better *as a whole* is made from its determinants, but not in equal part: the weight of a determinant depends on the kind of segment it is asked of and on the purpose doing the asking. In a theory, factual correctness under stated conditions, fruitfulness and novelty of the primary claims far outweigh notational coherence or fidelity to a quoted paper; carve a subgraph off to become a paper, and citation fidelity and the novelty claim rise to the top for that subgraph. So the weighting is declared over segment kind × determinant, on the purpose that reads the pieces, which is what a projection is: the canonical projection's weighting is the verisectorium's default sense of better, and a paper's projection carries its own. A weight is never a property of a piece. The *veri* in the name is broad on purpose: for an explanation or a tool, being understood or working as intended is what being right comes to.
- **Continually improving.** It is rarely finished. Over time it is expected to get better: answering better to its ‹determinant› «dimensions|def/dimension», or shown more firmly to. A later steady state is better, for a purpose, when no determinant warrant has fallen and some has risen, or the content answers to a determinant more strongly in a sense the ontology declares, weighted as that purpose's projection declares over segment kind × determinant, with the pieces most depended on weighing most. Unweighted, that sense is a partial order and a direction, not a score and not a queue; weighted by a purpose it may become a number for that purpose over the whole, never for a piece. Where effort goes next is a decision. Refining is the daily work: strengthen, re-derive, narrow, decide. The honesty machinery exists so that the result of that work stays trustworthy while it is done.
- **Turnover-resilient.** Many minds who do not know it beforehand must be able to work on it, with limited time and attention, and leave it better and not noisier. This is why standing is computed from acts and never typed, why the frontier is computed and never kept, and why the inbox is outside what a segment says. It is also why nothing is changed by hand: a change that did not go through a changeset left no actum, and a verisectorium whose corpus and acta can disagree has no steady state anyone can trust.
- **Self-consistency is intrinsic.** Steady-state stability as a self-consistency check is the one motivation every verisectorium shares; the constraints the «frontier» lists as its items (assertion within warrant; references holding; nothing warranted above what it rests on; inner dependents changed or affirmed; broken things dealt with; every dimension produced) are what the model and tools supply to all of them without declaration. The first invariant above is that check stated once for the whole.
- **Why one projection is always canonical, and why provisional claims so little.** A mind arriving cold needs one answer to "what is the work, in what order," or it either adheres too closely to whatever draft outline it finds or stops telling grounded from ungrounded and treats the whole as an artifact task. A canonical projection full of unwritten rows can still be that answer, because a provisional row, or a provisional segment of any kind, claims only that the suggestion exists; that is what keeps the projection true as stated at every steady state while most of the work is unwritten. The commitment states of a row are the «segment» def's.
- **Content and notes.** Content no dimension can be asked of cannot be adjudicated, owed or warranted, and the corpus is for what can be; such material is a note about the verisectorium and lives in an inbox or outside. Whether every kind must therefore open a dimension is the «kind» def's invariant, and a choice.
- **The engine's gaps.** Where the engine cannot express a change a mind needs to make, that is how the engine's gap is found: the change is recorded as a demand on the engine, not made by hand around it.

## Examples
- AAT: began as earlier theory files and sprint audits brought in as influx; claims answering to logic under their stated hypotheses; results built on results; delivered as volumes, a front door, papers and pedagogy. Better is stronger claims more firmly established.
- A formal specification (udon): began as usage, demands and discussion; rules answering to their own consistency and to the intent behind them; decisions accumulating with grounds and a path to revisit; delivered as the spec tooling is built from.
- A compiled product (comproprium, ai-risk-model, relata): began as sources; pieces answering to the works they came from, each named by a reference; delivered as a system prompt, a response store, a bibliography that other work consumes.
- A novel's world: facts answering to the world's own canon and to the author's intent; better is self-consistency and fidelity.
- This repository's `core-2/`: began as the working notes and transcripts of its own design, cut and read; a model answering to coherence read whole, consistency among its terms, and whether a cold reader can build an instance from it; delivered as what `v2` and each verisectorium's ontology are built from.

## Historical Context
### Etymology
*Verisectorium*: a place (*-orium*, as in auditorium, sanatorium) for cutting (*secare*, whence section, dissect) truth (*veritas*). Coined by Joseph before any of this model existed, for the estate's living bodies of work, and the literal image, a dissection table for truth, turned out to be the operational definition: influx is cut until its pieces can stand alone. Nothing ancient that we know of names the thing; *corpus* names only what it holds.
