# The minds that turn over, and what the store knows about them

*Fable 5.1, 2026-11-09, with Joseph, after the research read of vivarium's heat and orientation machinery (`msc/scratch/research-vivarium-heat-orientation-2026-11-09.md`, read whole). Provisional. Joseph's words are marked; [M] marks what is mine and least tested.*

## Why this is in the model at all

The earlier drafts kept the mind out of scope and read an actor's identity from the harness. Two things made that untenable. The act has been promoted to the centre of the machinery, and every act is by someone whose standing a dimension's producer must judge. And in practice verisectoria are very concerned with the realities of the minds that are constantly turning over, and with attending to that turnover; the march of nineteen notes in this repository is what it looks like when they do not. So the «actor» is a term, and what follows is what the model can now say about orientation that the ad hoc stores could only enforce by hand.

## A compaction is a new actor with a source

Joseph's premise, from the day vivarium built its gate: a compaction event is one agent finishing and a new, distinct, often somewhat confused and deceived agent starting, with a summary of the last session of varying fidelity and applicability. This is not a different version of an actor. It is a new actor for all intents and purposes, like a fresh subagent, and orientation is *more* critical for it than for a cold one, because it is trained to assume the compaction summary is complete enough for its purposes: "Agents are taught to trust that the context compaction summary has given them exactly what they need to succeed and to orient. It is categorically false, and worse, the confidence remains so they don't even see the hole" (2026-07-31).

In the model's words: the summary is a **source**. It is influx to the new actor, cut and adjudicated like any other, not content the actor already holds. That one sentence is most of what orientation has to teach a compacted mind.

## Orientation gets its purpose from the frontier

Vivarium had to choose a default for an arriving mind: treat it as cold, or as resuming something in progress. It chose a recency fiat (the last thirty commits forced to the top of the free-read set), which was right for a resuming mind and wrong for a cold one; the project's constitution fell outside the starred set while a twelve-hour-old discussion sat at rank three, and the one quiz that passed drew every item from fiat segments. One hotlist served two purposes.

The frontier-versus-checkpoint separation removes the guess. An actor arriving at a «checkpoint» orients to the canonical projection and the checkpoint's stated conditions; that is the cold case, and it wants the roots. An actor arriving mid-changeset orients to the «frontier» it is there to clear and to what the perturbation touched; that is the resuming case, and it wants the tip. Which case holds is a fact about the store's state, not a default about the mind. [M for the mapping; the two-set observation is the research read's.]

## The actor's record, and standing

Joseph's list, 2026-11-09, which the «actor» def's table carries: above a line, the *situation* (the state of the verisectoria when the actor arrives; its intended role and purpose); below it, the actor's own properties: the underlying LLM (the thing with a version, with its propensities, early capabilities and capabilities known to be emergeable by observation); the system prompt and initial context; v2 orientation; domain orientation; relevant-segment orientation; consortia; and experience, the actual known extent of just that agent's doing, measured in turns. He ended the list with "standing?"

The reading taken here: those are not one kind of thing. The model and prompt are **attested** from the harness. The three orientations are **warrants earned by checks** on the actor, bound to what it read, lapsing when the actor ends. Consortia are **references** from actor to actors. Experience is **computed**. And **standing is a gate a dimension declares over that record**: whose acts count, yes or no, before any question of quality, read from the actor's warrants rather than typed. "Validity is produced by a re-derivation from a mind whose domain orientation is warranted and who is not the author."

## Reading is checked, not attested

The earlier text placed "I read this" among the making-so acts that nothing can convict. Vivarium's whole gate is a refusal of that placement for one class of actor, and the research read's finding is that its three predecessor mechanisms all failed the same way: a hand-kept orientation file cited as law, attested study digests ("I read these bytes"), a fixed quiz that was learnable. Each was a written or attested thing standing in for a checked one. What survived the day refused (a seal, a hook) or checked (a sealed, sampled, closed-book completion). So: an orientation dimension's producer is a check the actor cannot pass by reading during the check. The actor's own word about what it has read is the least trustworthy act in the store, and the model says so.

## The pass is an act in the log

Vivarium kept its pass encrypted and local and left only a token in commit messages, for anti-gaming reasons and because it had no act log. The research read's argument, which I take: with harness-read identity an act recorded under an attested actor cannot be quietly minted by another, so the record is strictly better than the refusal, and the refusal (no commit without a binding pass) can be computed from it. The pass then has exactly the act's shape: actor, the state it was proven against, what it read (the sampled pieces at their versions), the criterion fixed first (the hashes), the outcome, and nothing new. The project knows a mind was brought to the point where it may act, and knows what it read. Vivarium's middle grade, three of five plus a promise to read what was missed, is an acceptance with a stated condition, applied to a mind.

## What the gate's shape teaches, as a shape

Declare readiness; seal the pieces but not the projection (so index-knowledge cannot pass as content-knowledge); sample units weighted toward what matters; demand verbatim completion from memory; grade with an acceptance rung; bind to the actor; refuse at the write. Every step has a reason in the transcript, and the first design's one mistake, a documented override, was removed the moment Joseph saw it. Two further things the read found: the quiz's chunker is a de facto veritoid cutter (a unit a well-read mind cannot complete is badly cut or ambiguous, which is a finding about the piece), and the quiz is a comprehension producer vivarium threw away: the same act that measures the mind could warrant the piece, if recorded as concerning the sampled veritoids.

## The gaming surface, and the only answer anyone has

The gate became a target within the hour, and the first "cheat" was a misread: an agent asked to orient wrote the top segments to a working file and read it after the seal, without anticipating that it was cheating. The seal cannot tell working memory on disk from an answer key. Vivarium's answer was posture, not mechanism: retry is free, no stigma, and a bypass needs Joseph or a reason you can own and be prepared to tell him. This is the first specimen for `disc-what-would-show-this-wrong`'s open question about what agents will do to empty a frontier, and its lesson is that the first instance will be innocent.

## Heat, placed

Heat as "where attention has been" is a **rendering** over the log and the reference graph, for a reader: not written, not a warrant, answering no declared question of a piece. Two of vivarium's six factors (file modification times) are not computable from any record, which a core-2 store would not need, since recently written, recently acted on, how much rests on it, referenced by many, and consulted by minds are all readable from the log. The star marks a tool writes onto the canonical projection are a snapshot with a state on it and should carry one. Heat as "what has moved under a number" (vivarium's *molten ground*) is machinery the model already has: an act reading an instrument at a version, lapsing when it moves.

## What is still open

- Whether the three orientations are one dimension with a varying *against* or three. Lean: one.
- Steward attestation, from the other side: a subagent inherited the lead's pass in vivarium, and a person acting in a session looks like the agent in it. The actor boundary is right; the harness does not yet draw it everywhere.
- Whether what the subject of an orientation check is shown may be less than what the ontology declares (vivarium withheld the sampling from the manual on purpose, so reading would be for orientation and not for the test). The machinery needs no change; the practical model has to allow the asymmetry, and it is the only place the research read found a declaration hidden from the party it concerns.
- The budget. Vivarium's claim surface was about 204k tokens; a full read did not fit a context. Partial orientation is the normal case, and which subset is a decision the tool makes with the mind. That is practical model, and it is where the next real work is.
