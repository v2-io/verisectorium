# Intake needs no machinery of its own

*Moved from the first-iteration overview (`MODEL.md`, Fable 5.1, 2026-11-08) into a segment of its own on Joseph's suggestion, so that it narrates the defs it rests on and lapses when they change. Provisional. Where a thing is Joseph's it says so; [M] marks the least tested.*

Taking material in is the first thing your definition says a verisectorium does, and it needs no machinery beyond the defs. A **source** is a ‹source› segment; its veritoids are **chunks**, of a chunk kind, and the cut must cover the whole source so that "every chunk dealt with" cannot mean "found something". A chunk carries a *bears-on* reference to the segment or veritoid it concerns. A ‹crossing› is an act that concerns a chunk and answers the dimension *where did it go*: changed an existing piece, became a new piece, already present, one more independent arrival (evidence, not a duplicate), superseded or rejected with a reason, delivered to another store, passed to a segment's inbox (not final). A segment's **inbox** is computed: the chunks bearing on it with no final crossing. The chunk lives in its source, so adding to or draining an inbox never changes the segment; that is the measured lesson of the sprint. "Read by whom, how far" are dimensions of the source, read from attestations of reading. A source is retired when every chunk has a final crossing, computed.

What sat in ASF's Working Notes was three things: inbox chunks; standing content that belongs in slots of its own kind (regression guards, open questions with closers, dead ends with reasons); and in-place fate markers written as prose ("Resolved 2026-10-30 in …"), which are crossings that never became acts.
