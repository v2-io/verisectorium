# Slot intents across the estate's verisectoria

*2026-11-10. A lossy roll-up of `segment-kinds.md`: every slot it shows in every segment kind, in every verisectorium, reduced to its bare name and grouped by what it is for. How a slot is carried (heading, frontmatter field, bold-labelled field, udon element or attribute, KDL node, YAML key, inline tag) and which store it is in are set aside; a slot called `Epistemic Status` in one store and `epistemic-status` in another are one row. Generated by `msc/scratch/proto-instances/segment-kinds/intents.py` (gitignored) from the same five territory passes.*

2241 distinct names occur across 108 stores; 850 of them in two or more. The names are exactly the slots `segment-kinds.md` shows; the ad hoc and one-off headings it folds are not here. Names used in two or more stores were placed by hand-written lists; names used in only one store were placed by keyword where one fits, which is looser. Names are reduced by dropping markup, numbering, trailing parentheticals and case, joining `-`, `_` and spaces, and taking the innermost part of a nested heading. Names that differ in spelling but not in intent (`depends` and `requires`, `scope` and `boundary conditions`) are not merged into one row; they meet under the same intent. Titles, ad hoc headings and withheld personal headings are left out.

**Columns.** *stores*: how many verisectoria use the name. *kinds*: how many segment kinds (store × kind). *occurrences*: files or units carrying it, summed across stores; it mixes files with records and entries, so read it as weight, not a count of anything one thing. *declared*: across those kinds, how often a store's own declaration marks the slot required, optional (including recommended and conditional), declared without either, or not at all. *carried as*: how many kinds carry it as a heading, a frontmatter field, a bold-labelled field, or a udon element. *what it's for*: the most common purpose the passes recorded.
