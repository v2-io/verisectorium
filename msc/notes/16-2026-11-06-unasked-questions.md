Here are the ones I think we have not asked. By the nature of the question I cannot see my own blind spots, so treat this as the list from where I stand.

**About whether the design is right**

1. **What would show this design is wrong?** Two days of design and no piece of real material has been through it. We have no stated prediction that a first trial could contradict. I would write one down before the first drain.
2. **What is the least that would have changed the sprint's outcome?** It may be three small things: knowing what has been read, an inbox outside the text, and findings going back to the same author. If so, how much of the rest is needed now?
3. **Is the store answering to anything outside itself?** A store can be perfectly honest internally and pointed in the wrong direction. You said on 10-30 that the theory's purpose itself was under review. Where does that question live?

**About what it does to the minds using it**

4. **What does a cold agent see and do in its first five minutes?** We designed what exists, not what is put in front of someone.
5. **What does each act of recording cost, and is it cheaper than not recording?** If structure is more work than prose, agents will write prose.
6. **What will agents do to make the frontier empty?** An author's affirmation and an "accepted" remainder are both cheap exits. Any number that marks "done" becomes a target.

**About the edges**

7. **What reaches you, and in what form?** Your attention is the scarcest thing in the system and the model says almost nothing about it.
8. **What can be shown to a skeptical outsider?** Everything so far faces inward.
9. **What happens outside AAT when a root changes?** The other volumes and the submitted papers rest on it.
10. **What makes two checks independent when nearly every checker is the same family of model?** The stopped-bound case says this is not a detail.
11. **How does anything leave, and is the surface shrinking?** We named the operations. We have no measure.

If I had to pick one, it is the first. The cheapest next step is still a single root drained by hand, with a written guess beforehand of what will go wrong.
