# The whole model, as small as I can honestly make it

*To Joseph, from the Opus 5.5 session of 2026-11-08, at your request. My proposal. Nothing here is ratified and none of it has been tried on a store. It uses plain words where it can, and the seven that carry weight are working names only. Where it comes from: your 11-05 dialog (note 05) and 11-06 statement (14); notes 09, 12 and 13; the census (18); and our conversation today. It uses the kernel spikes as evidence for nothing; one idea that first surfaced there is kept because it is useful, that an act's reads are separate from what it concerns. Marks: **[J]** yours; **[N]** from the notes; **[C]** from the census; **[M]** mine, the least tested.*

*What I was trying to do: say the whole thing in one sitting, with nothing defined only by reference to other definitions. Section 8 runs a real error through it, and section 10 says what would show it wrong.*

---

## 1. The whole picture

A verisectorium takes material in, cuts it into pieces, holds the pieces to declared questions, and assembles them into coherent wholes, under many minds who arrive knowing nothing. **[J]**

Two facts about it shape everything else.

It moves between checkpoints. At each one it is, in your words, "'good' or 'true', with stated conditions". **[J, 14]** Between checkpoints there is honest unfinished work, and the store can say exactly what is still owed.

Nothing it says about how well a piece stands is typed by anyone. Standing is read off a record of what was actually done. You called it "almost all an act+judgment machine". **[J]**

The store never holds truth. It holds what was written and what was done. A claim can be believed for good reasons and later fail, and that is not a malfunction. **[J, 15]**

## 2. What is written

**Veritoid.** A piece of content, the smallest thing a question attaches to: your "content with one or more dimensions + rules". **[J]** It almost never makes sense alone, and it is never changed alone. Its grain is discovered: when a check breaks part of it and not the rest, it is split. **[N]**

**Segment.** Veritoids written and judged together, under a name. It is why a change to one piece forces a look at its neighbours. A chapter, a volume or an outline is a segment whose places point at other segments, so the nesting needs nothing new. **[N]** At least one such segment is canonical: it says "this is the work, in this order". Its rows can be provisional, promised or present, and it stays true at every checkpoint because a provisional row claims only that the suggestion exists. **[J, 14]**

**Kind.** Says what a veritoid or segment holds and which questions apply to it. A segment's kind names its places, which are required, how many each holds, and which places depend on which inside the segment (the eli8 rests on the description, which rests on the formal claim). **[J]**

**Reference.** A written pointer from one veritoid to the veritoids it rests on, carrying the version it was last reconciled against. Someone declared it and it can be wrong. **[N]**

Everything in this section is written by an author. That includes what the census calls family E, the shape of a claim: under what conditions, how sharp, one direction or both, what it is not saying, whose it is. **[C]** Those are content, held in places a kind declares. Note 12's context, hypotheses, conclusion and derivation are one theory store's segment kind, not part of the core. **[N]** A statement a piece makes about its own standing ("exact", "conditional") is also written. The store's job is to compare it with what has been done.

## 3. What is asked

**Dimension.** One declared question. Your test holds: two things that can move independently never share one. **[J]** The census shows what it costs when they do: "valid" was at least eight questions, and "fidelity" has been three (an account of an event, a copy of a source, a restatement of the store's own body). **[C]** A dimension says four things:

1. **What it asks, and of which kinds of thing.**
2. **What it is relative to.** Faithful to what, understood by whom, important for what. Nearly every question has this second argument, and that filler is usually what moves. **[C]**
3. **What produces the answer.** Your "what builds/founds/sustains this axis?" **[J]** A script, a mind re-deriving, a run, a reader, someone with standing, the graph. A question nothing will ever answer is a wish, and it should be visible as one. **[C]**
4. **How the answer is read from what has been done,** and whether it passes along references.

| Asks | Of | Relative to | Produced by | Read as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Is each step right? | a derivation step | its premises | a mind re-deriving | the latest outcome that still binds: confirmed, narrowed, broken |
| Does the derivation deliver the statement? | a claim | the statement's exact wording, its neighbours | a mind testing it | the same |
| Do the quoted words match? | a quoted span | the source at a location | a script | pass or fail |
| Does it hold in simulation? | a claim | a simulator at a version | a run | highest rung reached, under that version |
| Does a reader get it? | an explanation | a reader of a stated kind | a reader | reader events |
| Who stands behind this? | a decision | the accountable party | that party | their latest word |
| How much rests on it? | anything | a stated target | the graph, plus a decision | computed |

Dimensions are not a flat set. In the working stores one can be a prerequisite for the meaning of another, take precedence over it in a conflict, or veto it outright. **[C]** An ontology should be able to declare that order.

The ontology is the list of a store's kinds and dimensions. It holds no content.

**Truth determinants** are the dimensions an ontology declares as what this work is answerable for, the ones "better" is measured by. **[M, from our conversation]** I lean to subset and not synonym, because importance, permission and promises are adjudicated too and none of them is an aspect of truth. **[C]** That is yours to decide. It needs no machinery beyond the declaration.

## 4. What is done, and how standing is read

**Act.** One recorded thing an attested actor did against the store: what it read, each at its version, and which of those it concerns. **[N]** The second argument of a dimension needs no slot of its own. It is among what the act had to read. **[M]**

An act names the dimension it answers. In your terms this is what makes it adjudication and not just activity. The census's plainest result is that "valid" hid at least eight different questions, and that passing checks and the breaking check were answering different ones. **[C]** A record that says only "checked, passed" cannot tell them apart.

Acts come in two broad sorts, and the difference matters:

- **Finding out.** A check, a run, a review. It has an outcome, which can be confirmed, narrowed or broken, and a later act can overturn it. A check should say whether its criterion came first. **[N]** Checks have standing of their own: a store may want to know whether this sort of check could have failed at all, since a suite can pass without ever being able to catch the thing. **[C]**
- **Making so.** A decision, an attestation, a ruling. "I decided this", "I read this". Nothing can convict these by running anything, and only the party who made one can correct it, or someone showing it never happened. **[C]** This is where an error is gravest. An agent's act recorded in your name is the failure this kind of act exists to prevent. **[J]**

**Value** is a dimension's answer for a piece, read from the acts that still bind. An act stops binding the moment anything it read changes, and it can say why. **[N]** So a ladder is one way of reading acts and a conjunction of named checks is another. Lowest-wins, or a credence where a store declares one, are others. **[M, from C]** This is why I would drop "scale" as a term: the shape of an answer is the shape of its reading.

Most kinds of absence become readings too. Nothing recorded means not asked. A dimension not opened for this piece is a stated fact. "Looked and did not find", "deliberately left open", "decided to be empty" are acts. **[M, from C]**

## 5. How it changes

**Changeset.** There are two sizes, and both are yours. **[J, 05]**

A *segment changeset* is a whole new written state of one segment at one logical instant. Every inner dependent is either changed or carried with the author's statement that it is still right. Otherwise it is a draft. An author's "still right" never counts as a check.

An *atomic changeset* is everything, writes and acts together, that takes the store from one checkpoint to the next. It usually takes many minds over many hours. Only the last write to each segment reaches the next checkpoint, and nothing exists between checkpoints, "simultaneously, logically speaking". **[J]**

**Checkpoint and frontier** are the line you said this work most has to draw. A checkpoint is a state the store actually rested in. Between checkpoints there is a working state, and it is never the verisectorium. **[J, N]**

The **frontier** is everything that would have to be true at the next checkpoint and is not, computed fresh after every write or act. It is not a list anyone keeps. Each item should say who moves next, since standing in the stores is mostly an obligation on the next mind. **[C]**

What goes on the frontier is what every verisectorium must hold, whatever it is about. This is your "internal self-consistency is an underlying current of all verisectoria": **[J]**

1. No piece states more about itself than its acts warrant.
2. Every reference holds: its target exists and has not moved since it was reconciled.
3. Every inner dependency is changed or carried.
4. Dependence has no cycles.
5. What a check or a finding says is broken has been dealt with.
6. Whatever an ontology adds. Its constraints are stated over dimensions.

Anything still unmet at a checkpoint is there by a recorded decision, with its reason.

A disturbance can be a write or an act. A finding that something is broken goes on the record before anyone has written a repair. **[N, 09]** So can a rule that fires, or an outside change such as a new simulator version.

How a value passes along a reference is declared per dimension. A premise's derivation status caps what rests on it. Freshness does not pass that way. A narrowed premise asks each dependent whether it used what is gone. **[N]** The census found several incompatible accounts of this in the stores, so I would not fix one rule for all. **[C]**

## 6. Intake

Taking material in is the first thing your definition says a verisectorium does, so it should not need separate machinery. **[J]** I had this as a leftover. I now think it falls out: **[M]**

- A **source** is a segment of a source kind, and its **chunks** are its veritoids. Cutting must cover all of it.
- A **crossing**, the record of where a chunk went, is an act that concerns the chunk.
- "Read by whom, how far" and "retired" are dimensions of the source, read from those acts.
- A segment's **inbox** is the chunks currently pointed at it and not yet finally crossed.

That last one gives what the sprint proved necessary. The chunk lives in its source and not in the segment, so adding to or draining an inbox never changes the segment. **[N]** A note written straight into an inbox is its own small source. **[N, 09]**

## 7. What "better" is

Steady is feasible: every constraint in section 5 holds, or is explicitly excepted. Better is a direction within that: answering better to the truth determinants, or being shown more firmly to. **[M, from our conversation]**

The distinction explains why softening is honest and still not enough. Lowering a claim is the cheapest way back to feasibility, your "low-energy keep the system stable change". **[J]** A store softened to tautologies would be perfectly steady and worth nothing. Strengthen-first is what the direction adds.

Two versions can each be better on a different determinant, with neither better overall. Choosing between them is a decision. The census's one specimen of scoring across questions scores candidates in a decision, not pieces in a store. **[C]** So I would keep composite scores off pieces. Which improvement to make next is also a decision, and it can weigh importance for some target.

Your two regimes need no machinery of their own. A theory claim is stated flatly and its dimensions read true or false. An empirical fact or proposed model is read by degree. They join at the hypothesis, where a flat if-then rests on a model whose adequacy carries the graded part. **[J, 12]**

## 8. A real case: the stopped bound

Prop A.1S(i), in `deriv-sector-condition`, marked exact, as the missed-error report records it. I have not opened the segment myself. The point is what the model would have recorded.

Say the ontology declares separate dimensions for "is each step right?" and "does the derivation deliver the statement as written?".

| When | What is recorded | What the store then says |
|---|---|---|
| Apr–Jul | Fourteen or so passes. Each is an act on step correctness: the Itô correction, the Grönwall algebra, optional stopping. | Steps: re-derived. **Delivers the statement: never asked.** The written "exact" is compared with both, so it is on the frontier from the start, or held there by a recorded decision someone can read. |
| Apr 25 | Audits break neighbour (iii) and recommend (i) as "the safe fallback". | A recommendation is not a check of (i), so (i) earns nothing from it. |
| May–Oct | The Working Notes say "RESOLVED in canon — do not re-open". | Closure has to be an act with an actor and a way to reopen it. A sentence in a note closes nothing. |
| Oct 30, 15:50 | A verifier of a new claim tests (i) against a neighbouring result. Outcome: broken. | That act answers "delivers", and it disturbs the store at once, before any repair. On the frontier: (i), and everything resting on it. |
| Same hour | Other checks confirm the segment on a different criterion. | They answer a different question and do not offset the break. |
| Oct 30, 16:29 | One changeset: (i) becomes a killed moment, (iii) is split, a new result arrives. | Acts that read the old text stop binding. Dependents go on the frontier and each is asked whether it used what is gone. |
| Then | Consumers are re-read. An independent mind confirms. | One dependent the report names was left open, and it stays on the frontier or is accepted with a reason. Then there is a checkpoint. |

What this shows and does not. The model would have made the gap visible for six months, but **only because the ontology split those two questions**. Declare one dimension called "valid" and this failure recurs exactly as it happened. So the model does not prevent it. It gives the honest place to put what was learned, and how well a store cuts its questions matters as much as anything here.

## 9. Where the rest of the outline goes

Seven terms carry the weight: veritoid, segment, kind, dimension, reference, act, changeset. **Value**, **checkpoint** and **frontier** are computed from them, and the ontology is a list of kinds and dimensions. Your decided term, verisectorium, is what they make up. The other outline rows would go this way:

| | |
|---|---|
| Computed | position, judgment, credence, ceiling and latent level, roll-up, orphan, whether a reference holds |
| Sorts or uses of the seven | the act subtypes, the premise changes, the recuts, projection, row, gap and planned segments, source, chunk, crossing, inbox, affirmation |
| Plain words, no term needed | log, state, content, version, name, actor, criterion, perturbation, accepted remainder |
| One store's choices | claim, context, hypotheses, conclusion, derivation, strength, sharpness, forced, the low-fidelity kinds, theory claim, empirical fact, proposed model |
| Still to place | ground, bound, standing, move and queue, delivery, rendering |

I expect some of these belong back as terms once they are used. The move I am proposing is that a word has to earn it.

## 10. What I am least sure of, and what would show this wrong

**Open, some of them yours:**

- How the definition of verisectorium should read. You are combining its senses (1) and (3) and deciding whether truth determinants belong in it right away or in a model overview. **[J]** This note assumes only what (1) already says.
- Whether a veritoid has an identity that survives being moved. Every split and drain hits this. **[J, N]**
- Whether a known defect shows on the checkpoint line before it is worked through. My lean is that a finding is visible at once and inhibits new reliance. **[J, still weighing]**
- Whether two independently steady stores combine into a steady one. You punted this. **[J]**
- What makes two checks independent when nearly every checker is one model family. **[N]**
- Whether questions can attach below a veritoid or to something outside the store. Spans, relations between pieces and other bodies all get adjudicated, and vivarium adjudicates minds. **[C]** My guess is that spans and relations can be handled by cutting and by reference, and that minds may be out of scope.
- Refusals. The strongest answers in the most-built store are read-only handles and the like, not records. **[C]** I think an ontology should be able to say "this is answered by a refusal", but I am unsure.
- Where a claim's status judgment lives. `exact` versus `conditional` records whether a premise was treated as inside the chain, and that judgment has no place of its own. **[C]** I would make it a decision act on the premise, so a later reader can disagree with it. This is the point I most want tested on AAT.

**A written guess, before any real material.** If one AAT root goes through this, I expect it to strain, in this order:

1. How many dimensions "valid" needs. Eight is too many to record per piece, and one recreates the stopped bound.
2. Per-part verdicts. Epistemic Status sections tier by component in 155 of 220 cases, so the veritoid cut will be finer than feels natural. **[C]**
3. The cost of recording. If an act costs more than prose, agents will write prose. The stores' dead fields say this will decide everything. **[C]**
4. What a premise is. The readers found eleven sorts of thing that could discharge one. **[C]** I do not know whether that is one reference with kinds or several things.

**This model is wrong if:** a real root needs a thing that is neither written, asked, done nor changed; or standing cannot be read from acts without someone typing it after all; or the seven have to multiply back to something like the eighty-five to do real work.
