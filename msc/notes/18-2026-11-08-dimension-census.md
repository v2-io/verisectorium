## In brief

About eighty separately named questions turned up across eleven stores, and I have sorted them by **what produces the answer**: comparison of two texts, derivation, contact with the world, an act by someone with standing, the author's own writing, computation over the store, or a fact about the mind or instrument doing the asking. That is Joseph's "what builds it" lens (JM line 810) applied to the census itself, and it put different things together than the first pass did. Eight things came out of laying the reports side by side. Each is argued in "What the census shows", with what it puts to the model.

1. **The questions recorded most are not the ones that hurt.** Effort goes where checking is cheap: relata has 259 bibliographic-field checks and 1 page check; ASF's 500 recorded checks fall on 94 of 1,234 records; the typed stage fields are filled at birth and almost never again. The failures the stores report cost were elsewhere: a check that could not fail, a word that travelled without its conditions, a sentence in someone else's voice.
2. **Three kinds of answer, not two.** The core already divides properties into written and computed. The field adds a third that is neither: an **attestation** (who decided, who read, what was predicted beforehand, whose eyes saw it), which can only be recorded and never convicted by running anything, and on which the stores put their heaviest moral weight. And the strongest answers in the most-built store are not records at all but **refusals**: a read-only handle, a compile-included ledger, a hook.
3. **Standing attaches below the record, across records, and into other bodies.** 472 of 790 ASF labels mint a name below the segment; vivarium reasons at the clause; relata's piece is a sentence in a paper elsewhere; a promise's standing worsens when its text is unchanged and the text around it claims more. Each store reader found the record grain wrong, from a different store.
4. **One field, several questions; the outcome kept, the judgment lost.** "Valid" is at least eight questions; `status:` was five, picked by four different rules; "fidelity" is three things; "verified" was a stage and a strength; `human_confirm` names agents. And the ASF word `exact`/`conditional` records the *outcome* of a judgment (is this premise inside the chain or outside it) with no place for the judgment, who made it, or whether a later reader agrees.
5. **Nearly every question has a second argument**, and it is the second argument that moves. Faithful *to* what, current *against* what, understood *by* whom, important *for* what, permitted *to* whom. Three readers, blind to each other and to the first pass, said this in their own words. What lapses an answer is usually a change in the filler, not in the piece.
6. **Absence has at least seven kinds and nearly every store reinvented them.** Not asked; not opened for this kind of piece; asked and unanswered; not applicable; looked and not found, with the search; deliberately left open, with the reason; decided to be empty; withheld from this reader. ai-risk-model's draft has seven more for sources alone.
7. **What the stores call standing is mostly an obligation on the next mind**: who moves next, how revisitable, who may close, what would reopen it, what a citer must carry, what must not be restored. A grade of how good a piece is appears rarely and is often refused outright.
8. **Checks have standing too**, and the record barely holds it: whether the check could have failed, which of the several "valid" questions it asked, what it read, whether the criterion came first, whether the checker shares the author's failure modes. The stopped-bound error was passed for five months by checks that each answered a different question from the one that broke it.

**What this census is not.** It is not news of what went wrong; Joseph knows the defects of the bespoke verisectoria first-hand and said so. It is an inventory of the *questions*, with where each lives and what it is made of, so the model can be checked against it. Nor is it a schema: the tools reader's caution stands, that this is "a catalog of what has been useful somewhere and not a schema for every piece", and neurips records that the register carrying its discipline reads to outsiders as padding (TOOLS §1, D2).

## The sort, and why this one

Each question below is placed by **what produces its answer**. The seven families, in order from most mechanizable to least:

| Family | The answer is produced by | Its shape | It lapses when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Two texts** | comparing a copy, restatement or pointer with its original | per anchor, pass/fail, re-runnable | either side changes |
| **B. Logic** | a mind re-deriving, constructing an instance, scanning for collision | an act with outcome confirmed / narrowed / broken | the text or a premise changes |
| **C. The world** | a run, a measurement, a firing, a reader, an eye | an event bound to an instrument at a version | the era, build or referent moves |
| **D. A party with standing** | someone deciding, attesting, consenting, permitting | a recorded act with a revisit path; never convictable | a named assumption fails; the party says otherwise |
| **E. The author's writing** | the author stating the claim's shape | written content; not a value | rewriting |
| **F. The store** | computation over what is recorded | attributes, roll-ups, lists | the recorded things change |
| **G. The mind or instrument** | facts about who asked and with what | preconditions on A–D | the mind's context ends; the instrument changes |

Joseph's own sort of 11-04 (JM line 810) cuts close to this: Certainty is B and C; Decision is D; Importance is F; his "(Hard)" states are A, C and F; "(Soft)" are C's reader and eye; "(Hard-enough)" is D's attestations and G. RC1's sort by what falsifies each axis cuts across both. I have not shown any of the three better; this one made the three-kinds finding visible, which the question-by-question sort of the first pass did not, and that is the reason I kept it.

A row says: the question, in plain words; what answers it; the shape the answer takes in the field; what makes the answer lapse; and one place to find a specimen, by report and section. **Mode**: *E* an explicit field or declared vocabulary; *P* a prose convention; *I* implicit in what was done; *R* a refusal or mechanism rather than a record. The L-rows of the first pass (the blind readers' additions) are folded in; its A–K rows are re-sorted, corrected where a reader corrected them, and in a few cases merged.

## What the census shows

Eight readings over the reports, each ending with what it puts to the model. They are mine, same-lineage, and tried on no real material.

### 1. Effort goes where checking is cheap, and the questions that hurt are elsewhere

The counts, from the readers: relata, 443 events, `bib-fields` 259, `anonymization` 76, `doi-resolves` 73, `claim-supported` 33, `page-ref` 1, while its own North Star is "what each work is and what it supports" (TOOLS §1, §5). ASF: 500 checks on 94 of 1,234 records, 74 of them on one segment; `stage:` 205 of 247 at `draft` and "currently ignored in practice"; vivarium 115 of 115 at `draft` with "no other value, ever"; comproprium 57 of 57; udon `verification-level` on 0 of 236 records (SURF H1; VIV §6; SRC §8; UDON §1). This repository's own outline has one accepted row and twenty-nine provisional, which is honest and is also the same shape.

Against that, what the stores report as having cost them: a suite green on a cached binary; `animate` that never animated; a `[Verified]` that verified the document; a "RESOLVED — do not re-open" note over a false result for five months; agent words carried as Joseph's; a confident false reason printed by a tool; a sentence in the wrong register adopted whole (TOOLS §4; MISSED §5; SRC §3.2). None of those is a bibliographic field or a stage.

The source-stores reader's inference, which I share: "fields that a tool computes or a mechanism forces stay accurate; fields that depend on someone returning later … stop on the day the founding energy does" (SRC §8). The udon reader: "A model of standing that depends on a clearing act nobody owns will report the state of its authors' attention" (UDON §5).

*What it puts to the model.* The core already computes values and refuses typed status. The census asks, per question in the tables: **who or what will ever produce this answer, and what happens to the store when nobody does?** A question with no producer is a wish, and the model should be able to say so of its own dimensions.

### 2. Three kinds of answer, and the strongest is not a record

«property» divides everything into written and computed, and «act» is the input both answer to. The field has two more things that fit neither.

**Attestations.** Who decided, who read it, whose eyes saw it, what was predicted beforehand, whose words these are. These are recorded acts, but they differ from a check in that nothing can ever convict them by running; their only check is another person's memory ("your memory of what you actually ratified is the reliable instrument, and the document's claim of ratification is the proxy", SRC §4.1 quoting comproprium). The stores put their gravest language exactly here (VIV B1; UDON §3.1's 2026-10-29 specimen: "I just don't want to pretend it's been fully adjudicated in our discussion, nor pretend that *I* asked anyone to change the cards when I haven't"). The core's «actor» is "read from the harness and never typed", which covers an agent's identity; the gap "steward attestation" is named in the outline; "I read it" and "I predicted this" have no place yet. Joseph's own sort has a bucket for these: "(Hard-enough) = e.g., source-agent-uuid, steward-at-that-time--- things that are attested to etc.-- that are asserted and assumed to be true but can still be corrected" (JM line 810).

**Refusals.** Vivarium's reader: "The strongest answers are not records at all. Vivarium's best-working mechanisms make the wrong state unrepresentable or expensive: a read-only store handle, a compile-included ledger, keys minted only through declarations" (VIV §1), with the project's own rule that "the exit criterion for any caught fault is a *test, lint, or refusing API* — not a paragraph". The orientation gate, `check-provenance`, the segment-changeset refusals the kernel spikes found necessary, and vsect's `confirm` refusing the reviser are the estate's other specimens. A refusal answers a question by making the wrong answer unwritable; it is neither a written nor a computed property.

*What it puts to the model.* Whether «value» has a third subtype beside position and judgment, an **attestation**, whose only mover is the attesting party and whose failure is not "wrong" but something the vivarium log calls grievous; and whether an «ontology» should be able to declare, per question, that it is answered by a **refusal** rather than a record, so that "is this a record, or a refusal?" is askable of every dimension. The vivarium reader's own phrasing: "A model built only of fields would inherit exactly the failure this project documented in its `stage` column."

### 3. Standing attaches below the record, across records, and into other bodies

Each store reader found the record grain wrong, from a different direction:

- **Below.** 472 of 790 ASF labels name a piece below the segment (LABELS); 155 of 220 Epistemic Status sections tier per component (ES-B); vivarium cites `#slug FE(4)` and has no field for it (VIV §2); udon's card holds "Joseph's words, an agent derivation he assented to, and an agent default he never addressed … at the same visual tier" (UDON §3.1); limen's verdict row holds "raise: confirmed · page follow: B2 · swap: noted" (TOOLS §5). Joseph's named dissonance (JM line 978) is this.
- **Across.** Relata's piece is an entry, but what is held to account is "a sentence in a paper elsewhere", and when that sentence changes nothing lapses (TOOLS §5). A promise's standing worsens with its text unchanged (`LOAD↑`). The check's criterion names "`:229`", "Item (g′)", "the same sentence in `#x`" while its key is the file (SURF §5).
- **Into other bodies.** Udon's `CONSUMERS.md`; ai-risk-model's pin of address theory at a commit; neurips's backport queue; a paper that is "the more careful of the two" against its own theory (TOOLS C3).

The core answers the first with «veritoid» ("the smallest thing questions attach to") and says grain "is discovered by adjudication and recorded as a split". The second and third have «reference» (veritoid to veritoid, with a version seen) and «delivery» ("Material sent on from one store to another").

*What it puts to the model.* Whether a «reference» can reach a veritoid in another store and lapse when it changes there; whether a relation itself (a citation-for-a-claim, a promise-to-a-companion) can be the thing that has a value, or only its two ends; and, from the tools reader, "the relation can sit in a different body from the record of it".

### 4. One field carried several questions, and kept the outcome without the judgment

The fusions, with their counts: ASF `status:` was "Eight words answering five questions" (note 15 via first pass), chosen by at least four rules (weakest claim, strongest with carve-outs, genre, ceiling; ES-A §4); "valid" is the eight questions of family B; "fidelity" is an account of an event, a copy of a source, and a restatement of the body (first pass, confirmed by SRC §4.8 and AUG Q17–Q18); "verified" in vivarium "meant both *"checked against the code"* and *"feels solid,"*"; `empirical` in ASF "carries at least four things across only 7 segments" (ES-A §6); relata's `human_confirm` is written for every `decide`, and 50 of 74 name an agent (TOOLS §5).

Reader B adds the sharper point. The `exact`/`conditional` word is drawn "by where a premise is written", not by inference strength, and "That judgement is what the status word is actually recording, and it is not recorded anywhere as a judgement — only its outcome is" (ES-B §2.2). Two groups of segments with the same shape of section take opposite words, and "nobody reading the words alone could tell the groups apart" (ES-A §6). The same loss appears in udon's second reads, where Joseph's hedges drop out of outcomes (UDON §3.1), and in the Claim Record, which "is starting to be that place by accident", the place where a judgment can live apart from its outcome (ES-B §7).

*What it puts to the model.* The core's «judgment» is "A value only judgment can give, held with its spread over the scale". The census asks whether the judgment that *assigns* a piece to a side of a line (premise inside or outside; this reading or that) is itself a recordable thing with an actor, so that a later reader can disagree with the assignment and not only with the outcome.

### 5. Nearly every question has a second argument, and the filler is what moves

Read the "Lapses when" column. Faithful to a source *at a location*; current against *a referent*; understood by *a reader of a declared kind*; important for *a target*; permitted to *a reader-role*; felt right by *the one person who can say*, in *his window*. Three readers reached this blind: ""How it stands" needs an object: stands in respect of what?" (SRC §1); "Standing here is mostly a statement about *what the next mind owes*" (UDON §6); "If the model's subject is "a piece of the body", a large part of this practice will have nowhere to live" (TOOLS §1). Joseph on importance: "always needs a target subject -- importance to/for *what/whom*" (JM line 802). The first pass had it as its Finding 1, and the AUDIT found its two supporting quotations were paraphrases wearing quotation marks; the finding survives without them.

What makes it more than a grammatical observation: the answer lapses when the filler changes, without the piece having changed. That is what an era is (VIV A4), what referent freshness is, what "whose eyes" is when the engine differs, and what the «truth·determinant» audit states as "what is held against can change while the determinant's text does not" (its finding 5).

*What it puts to the model.* The AUDIT of «truth·determinant» already asks this of determinants (a determinant naming a role that each piece fills through a «reference»). The census asks it of dimensions: whether a «dimension» is a question with a declared second slot, whether a «value» binds to what fills it as an «act» binds to what it read, and whether an attribute is simply a measurement with no such slot.

### 6. Absence has kinds, and nearly every store reinvented them

The list, assembled across readers: not asked; not opened for pieces of this kind (a declared empty ground, "never confused with nobody-has-asked-yet"); asked and unanswered; not applicable; looked and not found, with the search carried ("Absence claims carry their search"); deliberately left open, with the demand-side reason "so no future reader closes it in a framing the reason has already invalidated"; decided to be empty ("an empty evidence field is a finding about the practice, not a defect in the record"); hidden from this reader on purpose (TOOLS B2; UDON §3.7; VIV A6; SRC §4.4). ai-risk-model's source schema has seven more (lost in extraction; held elsewhere; withheld for hazard; redacted with reason; cited but not present; classified; published only as an average), "Each supports a different inference from silence". The NeurIPS checklist folds two of them into one `\answerNA{}`.

The founding specimen is comproprium's: "A check that finds nothing and a check that never ran produce the same observable: no output." The RC1 version is under-recording, which Joseph called "just as dishonest as an unwarranted lift" (JW, 2026-08-14 22:53).

*What it puts to the model.* The core has pieces of this: «ground» (not opened is a fact), «reference» (a missing target is owed, not refused), the «frontier». It has no typed null. Whether absence on a dimension is one thing or several, and whether the several are core or each store's.

### 7. What the stores call standing is mostly an obligation on the next mind

The udon reader's finding, which I checked against the other reports and found everywhere: flags say "who or what moves next"; `strength` is "how revisitable it is"; `decided-by` is defined by a "Revisiting it" column; closers say who may close; reopen-when says when; a regression guard is "a claim about the future behavior of editors" with "an addressee and a trigger" (UDON §6; WN §6). ASF's Epistemic Status sections are "addressed to someone who will arrive later and do a specific thing with this claim", and the fear under them "is consistent: **that a word will travel without its conditions**" (ES-A §13). Where a grade of quality would appear, the corpus refuses it: "No field or cell anywhere holds a hand-typed standing word such as 'verified', 'solid' or 'done'" (UDON §6).

*What it puts to the model.* The core has the «frontier» (what is owed, computed) and «queue» (the things waiting for one move). Whether a piece can carry a declared obligation addressed to a role (who moves next, what a citer must carry, what must not be restored) as something distinct from both a value and the frontier, and whether that is a «bound» or a third thing.

### 8. Checks have standing, and the record barely holds it

The stopped bound (MISSED §2) is the specimen: fourteen recorded passes over six months, each verifying a legitimate step (Itô correction, Grönwall algebra, optional stopping), none asking whether the statement followed from the steps; the break came from a verifier of a *new* claim placed beside the old one, under a brief whose one quoted sentence was "Re-derive from what is written rather than from what you can tell was intended". Two of the passes were not neutral: the audits that broke the neighbour (iii) "recommended (i) as the safe fallback". And "The same model saw it immediately" is "true of one instance with a re-derive brief and false of at least one other instance of that model the same morning".

What the record holds about a check today: an actor as a free string; a `criterion` as free text, "seldom read", one word for four things (SURF §4); `committed: before|after`, undefined anywhere an agent is pointed; `read:` on 139 of 500; a `role` that cannot say whether the actor wrote the thing. What the stores have learned to ask and have no field for: could this check have failed (limen's mutation checks, "a gate that tests its own list"); which of the eight "valid" questions did it ask; what did it look at ("the question to ask of any verifier is not *is it independent* but *what is it looking at, and who decided that*", SRC §4.4 quoting comproprium); does it share the author's failure modes ("Same-model "assessed" rungs are correlated opinion"). Vivarium holds its probes to this standard by name: "A green probe certifies only what its discrimination covers" (VIV A2).

*What it puts to the model.* «act» has criterion, reads and concerns, and the outline names "independence of acts" as a gap. The census asks whether a ‹check› must declare **which question it asked** as a countable thing (a member of family B, not free text), whether an act can carry its own sensitivity (has this kind of check ever been seen to fail), and whether independence is a predicate over declared reads and descent, as vsect's design has it (AUG Q41).

## Where the material disagrees with itself, or with the current core

1. **Fidelity is three things.** An account of an event, fixed at capture; a copy of a source, which rises with checking; a restatement of the store's own body. All called fidelity. (First pass; SRC §4.8 adds that comproprium attests a quotation by its speaker, not its extractor; AUG Q18 asks whether substrate fidelity is a gate rather than a dimension.)
2. **Is a usage licence earned?** RC1 calls it a bound, "declared, not secured by degrees", and in the same cell "projected from Evidence outcomes + Backing declaration". The core's «bound» has "no degrees". ai-risk-model's quote levels are "Earned, not typed": level 2 certified by a script, level 3 by a recorded act, and "tools may downgrade a mark that no record supports" (SRC §4.10). So at least one kind of licence in the estate is earned.
3. **Weakest link, max, gcd.** Vivarium folds weakest-link at the declaration grain ("Algorithm quality never launders inputs"); ASF's text holds three incompatible theories of how status passes along a use, and 30 of 1,142 `depends:` edges run from `exact` to something weaker (ES-A §7); Joseph names max and gcd as "two very worthwhile but distinct patterns"; the kernel spikes proved ceiling = min(position, min over targets) with uniqueness on an acyclic graph (`core/def/claimed-level.md`). Reader A's caution, from the corpus's own theorem: `depends:` is "a heavily reconvergent DAG", and propagation along it is exact only on an AND/OR tree.
4. **Every kind answers to a determinant.** On a tool, `DEBTS`, `VERDICTS`, the orientation gate and the consumer registry "would be notes on that test, and they are most of what carried limen and udon" (TOOLS *After*). The «truth·determinant» audit raises the same against sketches and reflections (its finding 9).
5. **Decided-by carries almost nothing, and is the gravest field.** Udon's second reader: "`decided-by` now carries almost nothing for lite's spec records beyond the word line" (UDON §3.2); vivarium: "THIS IS THE ONE FIELD WHERE AN ERROR IS NOT MERELY WRONG". Both are true; the field's value and its honesty are different questions, which is Finding 2.
6. **The body of a segment is both written and a record of acts.** ES prose "is written content about the claim" and holds "checks that were done, in prose" (22 sections) and history (19), against FORMAT's present-truth rule (ES-B §3.6). Those parts have no home once acts are recorded elsewhere.
7. **Stage.** ASF's is "Orthogonal to epistemic status" and three segments cap their status by a dependency's stage anyway; vivarium's is "a current-state marker, not a gate"; udon's doc-state "is not a ladder" (ES-B §6.5; VIV §6; UDON §4). The core has «frontier» and «queue» and no term for process state as such; it may need none.
8. **Rules that never fire.** FORMAT's "removed or emptied when the segment reaches `candidate` stage": zero segments at `candidate`, ever (WN *Short answers*). Vivarium's `Kept` is never reachable because predicates are prose. Udon's `awaiting-second` is never cleared. Each is the same shape: a transition whose actor was never assigned.
9. **Two readings of the core that the first pass got wrong, per the AUDIT.** «truth·determinant»'s Working Notes do lean on an order (a bound at conflict). «claimed·level»'s own Working Notes already say the field may be "one field or several". The tension between the singular term and ASF's labels is acknowledged inside the term, not only by its neighbours.
