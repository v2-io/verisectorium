# What the guesses came to

*Corrected after the independent read; see `08-corrections-after-the-independent-read.md`.* *2026-11-11, spike lead. `02-guesses-before-running.md` was written before any tool was installed. This says what became of each, from the per-instrument files, which I read whole. One caution on reading it: the three forks were forks of me, so they started with these same guesses in mind. Where two instruments agree below, that is one mind's guess surviving two different tests. It is not two minds agreeing.*

| | the guess | what happened | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| G-D1 | "written, done, computed" is Datalog's stored and derived; the tools enforce it; stored facts split by how rules read them, and that split is the spec's base and line | **Overturned in its strong form.** The theory draws the line; clingo and Soufflé both accept a status typed by hand beside the rules that compute it. The split by how rules read a fact is real, and it is *not* the spec's base and line: an affirmation and a waiver read like evidence. | `datalog/PERSPECTIVE.md` §2.1, §2.4, §4 |
| G-D2 | consistency is the absence of fault, and then nothing is chosen | Held, on the ring, under three engines. Lean then proved it for any number of pieces and proved it equal to the greatest fixed point. | `datalog` §2.2; `lean` §4 |
| G-D3 | the three pathologies give 0, 2 and 1 stable models; undefined, undefined, false | Held for the first two. The ring gives one model when written as fault, and **two** when note 22's condition 5 is transcribed literally, which I had not predicted: the non-uniqueness the builds measured comes from the direction the sentence is written in. | `datalog` §2.2, §2.3 |
| G-D4 | "unconditional" is blunter than needed; the exact line is no cycle through a condition | Held on toy programs and on one story built into M0. Not predicted: a stratified engine refuses even the harmless case, with the same error as the paradox. | `datalog` §2.3 |
| G-D5 | a waiver conditioned on consistency is harmless to well-definedness | Held, and Lean found the same thing by its own test: it accepts the conditioned waiver and refuses the conditioned questions. What the greatest fixed point then accepts is a defective piece standing on a circular waiver. | `datalog` §2.3; `lean` §5 |
| G-D6 | good news can be read off the rule graph | Held in part. For the flag that evidence was recorded and holds, given its description: yes. For the whole event the same reading says "either". The reading is coarse (relation names, not arguments). It recovered the Ada account's G8 without running a state. | `datalog` §2.4 |
| G-D7 | Datalog lands on "a line is a set, a state is as of a time, closing is union" | Held. And went further than guessed: see the last row. | `datalog` §2.8, §2.5 |
| G-L1 | three tiers; nearly everything is in the third | Held. Not guessed: the states of an empty slot are what make the first tier possible. | `lean` §9 |
| G-L2 | a checkpoint that cannot be built without a proof | Held. | `lean` §2 |
| G-L3 | **same** as a view; normalization, graded reads and attested sameness are one thing | Held as a construction beside M0. It forces a choice: an equivalence, not "close enough". Build systems reach the same idea (early cutoff on a function of an input). | `lean` §8; `03` §4 |
| G-L4 | Lean refuses a question conditioned on consistency | Held, by both of its checks. | `lean` §5 |
| G-L5 | Lean has no native way to say "owed" | **Wrong.** It says it easily, as a function. What it refuses is evidence in the place of proof. | `lean` §3 |
| G-L6 | small theorems at any size | Held for three: fault as reachability, good news for M0, the skeleton of the frame property. | `lean` §4, §6, §7 |
| G-T1 | fork-level steps refine checkpoint-level steps | Held, nearly by construction. The content was in what had to be made visible. | `tla` §1 |
| G-T2 | learning must be a visible step or R11 fails as an invariant | Held: refuted in two states as an invariant, holds in 2.5 million as "as of its last close". | `tla` §2 |
| G-T3 | interleavings reproduce the clock case | Held, and found a second fault I had not guessed (two rewrites of one piece merge silently) and a third observation (under merging by time the main line's past changes). | `tla` §5 |
| G-O1 | assumption-based truth maintenance already holds "what was learned on an abandoned line is still known" | Held, from secondary sources. A run in its spirit: the base alone determines which combinations of existing versions could be rested at. | `03` §3 |
| G-O2 | verifying traces and early cutoff are evidence-with-reads and **same** | Held, from secondary sources. Not guessed: build systems have no guard, and seeing why says a guard is a regression test. | `03` §4 |
| G-O3 | the texts say more than "read set against write set" | Held and sharpened, from the primary: the builds' R9 is *invariant confluence*, with a theorem that where it fails, waiting is unavoidable. | `03` §1 |
| G-O4 | the base-and-line split is the CALM split | **Half wrong.** The base growing is the coordination-free part. But the frontier is not monotone in the base, which is why "stationary" can only be said as of a time. | `03` §2 |
| (none) | | **Not guessed by me at all, and the thing I would put first:** on a line merged from two forks, nothing owed should be read from an event's position. Three faults there (the clock case, the lost rewrite, a guard answered by the other fork's pass). I first claimed four and a wider conclusion; two of the four were faults of the guard's wording. | `datalog` §2.5; `tla` §5 |
| G-A1 | with obligations unconditional, consistency needs no fixed point, and the whole computed side can be written with closures only | Held in M0 at small sizes: three reachabilities (canon, sameness, fault) and nothing recursive.
