## How to read an entry

**Where.** `NN:line` means `msc/notes/NN-*.md` at that line. `17b/` is the first core (`msc/notes/17b-2026-11-07-first-core/`), `08/NN` is a file in `08-2026-11-05-reading-notes-fable/`, and `15` and `17` are the two handoffs in `.archive/`. `27.feedback:` and `26.feedback:` are the two `*.feedback-1.md` files beside notes 27 and 26. `working:` is `msc/scratch/vision-2026-11-21/00-working.md` and `addendum:` is `msc/notes/30-addendum.md`. Core files are given by path. Lines in these files are often whole paragraphs, so a line number pins a quotation closely. When a note was written: 01–02 on 11-04; 03–10 on 11-05; 11–16 on 11-06; 17b on 11-06/07; 18–20 on 11-08; 21 on 11-09; 22–23 on 11-10; 22a, 24 and 25 on 11-11; 27–28 on 11-18; 29–30 on 11-19; the addendum on 11-20/21; the working file on 11-21. Core's Working Notes were written between 11-08 and 11-10. **Nothing in `core/` has changed since 11-10** (its last actum is 2026-11-10T20:38), so "carried open in core" means parked since then at the latest.

**Ground.** This is the distinction Joseph's remark pointed at. Each question is marked with what stood behind it when it was asked:
- **case**: a real store, a failure, a count or a measurement;
- **build**: a formal build, a kernel spike, a typed language refusing to go on, or an instance built from core;
- **J**: Joseph's own experience or words;
- **worked**: an invented example that was run through the model;
- **bare**: asked in general terms, with nothing concrete behind it.

**Fate.** One of: *answered* (where, and by whom: Joseph, or a lead whose answer he has not confirmed); *carried open* (with the latest place it is carried); *lost* (with the last place it appears; "lost" here means I searched later files and did not find it, and says which); *restored* (lost, then brought back).
