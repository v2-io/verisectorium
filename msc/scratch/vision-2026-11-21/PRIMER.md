# Primer: the picture, told once

*To Joseph, and to a mind arriving cold. Written 2026-11-22 00:10 by the session that wrote `00-working.md`, after Joseph said the working file's short summary had kept the conclusions and lost where the weight is. The working file is a log: attempts, then corrections in the order they came. This is what it adds up to as I hold it now. It is one evening's understanding and I expect it to be superseded. Sources and their strength are in the working file and in the four footnoted reports beside it.*

## The centre

The understanding of what a body of work is, and of how to extend it correctly, lives in a person. Here that is Joseph. Naur said this of programs and Thurston of mathematics. This project's own files show it: questions dropped between notes because the next mind had not read the last one, and this session re-derived a three-week-old review and three of Joseph's earlier statements without knowing.

Joseph's own estimate of costs (2026-11-21; 1 cheapest, 10 dearest, roughly logarithmic) says where that bites: an agent reading 1, writing 2, really understanding 3, orienting cold 3 to 5; agent and steward coming into alignment 5; the steward reading 4, understanding 5 to 6, **writing 6 to 7**, and a human orienting cold 10. So the dearest channel in the system is the one the irreplaceable input travels through: his understanding becoming words.

What worked on 11-19 to 11-21 went around that cost. He did not write the model down. He read a phrase and kicked it in a line or two, and each kick moved the model. A large part of the design problem is how what he knows gets in at the price of a reaction and not an essay.

## Two currents

**Decay.** Whatever one mind rewrites from its reading of another's account loses something, and comes out sounding surer and less conditioned than its source. Joseph's theory predicts the loss (an ASF segment on turnover). Three unrelated fields measured the false sureness. This repository's notes show both.

**Inflow.** Four things put something back, and they are not interchangeable:

1. *The world:* a new source, a run, a measurement. Only this says whether the work bears on anything.
2. *A stored original,* kept verbatim. It restores what a rewriting lost and no more than it holds.
3. *The steward:* what he knows and what he wants.
4. *What the arriving mind itself brings.* Joseph added this, and I had left it out: an agent's training is a very wide body of distilled knowledge that the work does not hold, and its cross-domain connections are a real contribution, in time possibly a unique one. The outside ancestors and parallels found on 11-21 (Parnas and Clements, Naur, Thurston, Lakatos, the handoff studies) came in this way. Like any inflow it arrives unadjudicated: plausible until something checks it. Joseph also named its floor: even with nothing special about any one mind, many varied attempts are a brute-force search, "as long as there is a way to filter truth out of the noise". That form is worth what the filter can absorb, and attempts from one model family vary less than their number suggests.

Doing a derivation again is not on that list. It catches slips and brings in nothing.

The machinery in note 30 (pieces, kinds, outlines with a reader, a record of what was done, readings computed from it) is plumbing for recording which inflow touched what, and when. That part looks close to settled. Four pilots converged on it, and it has a 1986 ancestor that ran thirteen years at a nuclear plant and was given up for cost.

## What weighed most

- **Under turnover the machinery takes over from the ways of thinking** (Thurston). It describes every lead Joseph has corrected.
- **The pull toward more terms, conditions and recorded detail is the "more detail" reflex** in Joseph's own notes on Bungay (note 26), where the remedy is a stated what-and-why, limits, and having it said back. An earlier agent had called that note irrelevant to this work.
- **"The wrong universe was pushing back."** Joseph's words. The leads who multiplied terms were being pushed on by formal builds and consistency reads, which find edge cases without end and cannot say which matter. Use says that.
- **His test for a factoring:** minds who lived in the stores would say it would have made things nicer. An agent can borrow some of that by replaying recorded episodes from a real store through a proposed idea.
- **Terms come after cases.** The cycle is a curve of complexity that peaks when the right questions are formed. On 11-21 he judged the machinery just right of the peak, and agreed that what a record cannot carry is still climbing.
- **His unease is usually a sense of unasked questions.** They were found on 11-21 by letting something outside the statement push on it, and not by asking what was missing.
- **Tying each claim to a quotation found 29 errors** in four reports that lists of sources read had missed.
- **Handoffs that work are conversations, and what is passed is stance.** The model has no place for it. (`outside-fields.md`, section 5.)

## Dead ends, kept visible

- **Attention as the one quantity** that explains everything. It helps compute what a piece is worth to a reader. It is not what the work is for.
- **The model "is" a database.** I wrote that as a conclusion in the addendum. Joseph, answering an earlier and milder remark of mine and not knowing I had written it, said the database reading is a perspective and not a definition. He had anticipated where I would go. The addendum's section 12 now says so.
- **"Contact" as a single idea.** It split in three the first time a primary was quoted against it.
- **"Reading is the scarce thing."** By Joseph's scale agent reading is the cheapest act there is.
- **Arrival by attempting in place of reading.** The one study behind it also found good documentation very valuable.
- **"Nobody who is good at cutting can say how."** Two practitioners and one argument.
- **That his slow reading of note 30 was a bottleneck.** By his own theory a misleading phrase in the one statement every mind reads costs most.
- **That the notes repeat the failure core was meant to stop.** Too broad; what goes wrong is that each is written in a finished voice from the one before.

## Least understood

How the work moves forward. Nearly everything above is about not losing ground. Two things bear on it: the fourth inflow, and room. Thurston held proofs back so that others had something to find. The estate's practice of not prescribing a delegate's method does the same job, and "open at the edge" is where a body of work would record that room.
