## What I take from it for the spine
This is the first note where the model's center of gravity is in Joseph's voice rather than an agent's. Three things he insisted on, each of which the agent-built models had missing:
- **Identity and fate are different units** (segment vs atom), and the grouping exists *for adjudication*: you keep atoms together because you have to adjudicate transitions of their dimensions as a coherent whole. The group is not navigation, it is the unit of coherent change.
- **A kind must say what content it has.** Templates are content layouts with cardinalities, not type-tags.
- **Change is the primitive event**, at the grain of a coherent instance, and the system's job is to say what is still inconsistent while a larger change is in flight. Steady-state vs intermediate-state is the distinction the mechanism must preserve.

And the unresolved thread that I think is the spine's hard part: dependencies are *at a ladder level*, ceilings propagate, and whether a value is a "level" at all depends on the ladder type. That is where "act+judgment machine" vs "numeric truth value" will have to be decided.
