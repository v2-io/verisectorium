## What differed
I predicted A0–A8 as a formalization of §7. True in outline, but the plan is far more ambitious than §7 and goes somewhere §7 didn't: a full **credence calculus**. A0 fixes four primitives (truth claim, decision, attested fact, attribute) and then *four quantities kept apart*: position (a trail fact), credence P(true) in [0,1], judged value (a distribution over an aspect's scale), forecast (what a re-check would find; never a guard). A2 then builds: weight-of-evidence in Good/Turing log-likelihood-ratio form; independent evidence adds, dependent counts once; refutations cap lexicographically; self-checks have LR ≈ 1 (116 self-checks found nothing where independent checks found defects 42% of the time); Dawid–Skene for checker error rates needing a calibration subpopulation with three independent checks per version; eras when calibration changes; premise conjunction via Fréchet bounds; depth attenuation (0.95^20 ≈ 0.36; roots reach ~245 segments); notify-don't-cascade with band hysteresis load-bearing at roots.

This is the "WoE and numeric truth values" the index's transcript-only exchange says Joseph later suspected he introduced prematurely. So I am now reading the thing that gets retracted. It reads, from here, as beautifully worked and almost entirely unexercised: every one of its numbers comes from the sprint trail, and every mechanism (LR estimation, calibration subpopulation, ordinal Dawid–Skene, eras) is a thing that would need building before a single record could carry a credence. The memory file's warning fires hard: A0–A2 is a term-lattice defined against itself (position/credence/judged value/forecast/basis/graduation/band/era), and the one place it touches ground is the sprint statistics.

## Wander
The deep tension I see: the plan has two spines competing. One is **act-shaped**: records, events bound to versions, direct-read binding, lapse with cause named, closed-world trail, one current label. Everything there is a fact about what was done. The other is **belief-shaped**: credence, LR, calibration, forecast, judged-value distributions. The act spine is cheap and needs no estimation; the belief spine needs a statistics program before it says anything. The index's quote ("almost all an act+judgment machine") reads as Joseph choosing the first spine. My guess for where the theory's spine lands: the record of acts is primary, and any number is a *projection* over acts, declared and replaceable, never stored as standing. That's A0's own "position is computed from the trail" generalized, with credence demoted from primitive to optional projection.

Also noting: "claimed vs established" from 01 §7 survives into A2 but is reframed: adjudication usually *finds* the mismatch rather than starting from it. Good correction.
