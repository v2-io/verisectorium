**File 09, RC1 read whole.** The prior Fable's verdict, which I find persuasive and which I could not have produced without RC1: RC1 is "a theory of one piece of content under one question at a time. Record × dimension × event." Its good constraints live there (dimension as question + degrees of warrant + dishonesty modes; substitution law; status computed as of a moment; declared foundation opens the lines; coherence default; commitment law; rebut vs undercut; re-pricing as recompute+notify+threshold; references with intended cardinality; designator vs description; bounds; disposition as placement-for-influence; decline events and the *expressive requirement*: every honest partial position needs a cheap sayable form). What it lacks is any unit above the piece, in structure (no whole that pieces belong to; roll-up rule left open in 03, asked in Q7, no declaration form in 09) and in time (no act spanning pieces; the field trial found EPISODE from outside). "These are the same omission. A whole in structure is what gets written together; a write that spans parts is what makes them a whole." And the framing choice that hid it: defining the unit by how it fails cuts ever finer and never asks what pieces are for; organized around support, the whole appears because support inside a segment is dense and across segments sparse, "and that difference in density is what a segment *is*." Also: RC1's LAND (identity persists, body replaced, edges keep resolving) vs 02's OBO rule (new identifier); the Fable leans RC1. And a real conflict within RC1 itself: 02 says adjudication grain = record grain; 03 says every dimension can attach below the record.

**File 10, the time correction.** Joseph said 3.1 did not represent the segment changeset and the atomic changeset "with practical sequentialness but logical simultaneity." The repair: **two kinds of time**. Steady time: the store moves S0→S1→S2, each step one atomic changeset, all its changes logically at one instant, nothing exists between steady states. Work time: the hours in which minds assemble the step, ordered, clocked. A state = which version every veritoid is at; the working state = last steady state + what's staged. Three recorded things: a segment changeset (a new whole state of one segment, parts unordered), an act (performed against a state, binding to exact versions, possibly spanning segments), an atomic changeset (the container that moves steady time). Worked example with a frontier table. Content is reconciled by the author at one instant ("changed or affirmed"); values are not the author's to set, they drop when the changeset stages and come back one act at a time; that owed work *is* the frontier. The Fable withdraws recursion as the working model: two levels, leaf (content + dimensions, = veritoid) and segment (named ordered set of leaves in declared slots, with aliased/derived/own dimensions); recursion kept only as mathematics, where Joseph put it. Two things left open, both Joseph's: may a finding be visible on the steady line before its consequences are worked (the known-defect mark; "consistent and honestly current" as two different promises), and acts with no content change.

The RC1-read also surfaced that udon's address theory was built for exactly this ("allowing document parts to be a graph essentially, with event-sourcing like temporal dimension"), and that the best existing exemplar of a well-formed multi-part thing is an address-theory def term-group, not an ASF segment.

## What I take for the spine
By the end of 11-05 the model has, in my reading, five load-bearing moves, all Joseph's and all in his words somewhere in 05 or 08:
1. Support, not fate, is the organizing relation; fate is derived (reachability along support).
2. Content + dimensions is the leaf; the leaf is meaningful only in its segment; the segment is the unit of coherent adjudication and of a write.
3. Two times: steady and work. The store is only ever "the theory" at steady states; between them it owes a frontier.
4. Content vs values: authors reconcile content at an instant; values are computed from acts and are never typed.
5. Practical granularity over a recursive ontology; terminology last.
