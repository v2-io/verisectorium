## What this move showed

- **The spec's real defect never reached the frontier.** e1 was wrong from S0, and it stayed out of canon only because its acceptance rule was strict (both means). Being strict about acceptance had the effect of hiding the error. A looser rule (one means) would have accepted e1 on the parser's word at S0, and then the contradiction at step 1 would have been owed. The steady state looked cleanest in the configuration where the error went unseen.
- **An instrument as a "means" was the wrong declaration.** The parser run answers a different question ("what does kvlp@0.3 output?"), held against a different thing (the parser), and declaring it a means of *conforms* turned a parser bug into an example defect. The stopped-bound lesson, that means must be declared, holds for minds. For instruments it needs a test: a means is only a means if it is held against the dimension's own *against* (finding 8).
- **The waiver at step 8 is for work that is "merely elsewhere"**, which «steady·state» says waivers are not for. There was no alternative inside the model. The honest alternative is an ontology change (*conforms* read as hand derivation alone while no instrument has standing), and where that change is recorded is a gap row.
