# The model in a nutshell

A reading path over `core/`, not canon: it orders parts of the canonical segments for a reader who wants to hold the whole model, and may add segments of its own under `nutshell/`. Nothing is copied here; `bin/nutshell` assembles the text from the rows, and `bin/nutshell check` says whether every row resolves and how long the result is. The aim is a text no longer than the one the model was first explained in (about three hundred and fifty lines), with each word met before it is used.
