# The whole thing, first

*Moved from the first-iteration overview (`MODEL.md`, Fable 5.1, 2026-11-08) into a segment of its own on Joseph's suggestion, so that it narrates the defs it rests on and lapses when they change. Provisional. Where a thing is Joseph's it says so; [M] marks the least tested.*

A «verisectorium» is a place for cutting truth. Every one in the estate began as a pile of influx (quotes gathered on a subject, spikes, drafts, results, an older theory's files) and was dissected until each piece could contribute on its own, adjudicated by recorded acts, refined toward agreement with what it is held to, and reassembled into coherent results that others read and build on. That production is the purpose; results built on adjudicated results are how discovery and decision proceed, and how work compounds under turnover instead of being redone. Concretely a verisectorium is an ontology, a corpus of what has been written, acta of what has been done, a history, and praxes for the minds that work in it.

The five sentences that follow are the **machinery** that keeps that production trustworthy. They are general, and content divides under them without end in principle while stopping in practice at a few scales. The **practical model** a mind at work meets (an outline with marked rows, document-like segments as markdown, an inbox, a frontier, a steady state) is built on this machinery and is not the same thing as it; the «verisectorium» def says more. Everything after this row of the outline is these five, made exact enough to run.

1. **The corpus holds what was written and the acta what was done. Everything it says about how well a piece stands is read from that record, never typed from outside it.** It holds many true sentences, but it holds no verdict on them except what the acts warrant: a true claim nobody has checked and a false one everyone has passed look the same to it until an act says otherwise. A claim believed for good reasons that later fails is not a malfunction.
2. **A dimension is a constraint.** For every piece and every question its kind opens, what the piece *asserts* about itself may not exceed what the acts *warrant*, and what passes along its references must hold. A violation is owed. This is your "dimensions become logical correctness constraints", and your "internal self-consistency is an underlying current of all verisectoria" is the set of constraints every verisectorium has whether or not it declares them.
3. **A steady state is a state where nothing is owed, except by recorded acceptance.** Between steady states the verisectorium is in work time, and the frontier says exactly what is still owed and who can clear it. The acceptances are the steady state's stated conditions.
4. **Writing happens in segments, at one logical instant; warrant moves one act at a time.** There are two levels of content, veritoids and the segments they are written in, and no more.
5. **Better is a direction along the dimensions the ontology names as determinants.** It is a partial order, not a score and not a queue. Softening is the cheapest way back to a steady state; strengthen-first is what the direction adds and is the daily work. What to work on next is a decision.

And one rule under all five: **identity is minted and outlives content.** Acts bind to versions; versions are of something; that something has an id minted for it and never reused.
