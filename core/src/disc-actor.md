# The minds that turn over: orienting them

The «actor» def says what an actor is, what its record holds, and why a compaction mints a new one. This segment is about what follows for orientation, which is the first thing a verisectorium does for every mind that arrives.

## Orientation gets its purpose from the frontier

Without the difference between settled and in-work, a verisectorium has to guess what an arriving mind is: cold, or resuming something in progress. The two want different things to read, and one list cannot serve both; a list weighted toward what moved recently hands the resuming mind what it needs and hands the cold mind the predecessor's momentum instead of the roots.

With the difference, the guess goes away. An actor arriving where nothing is open orients to the canonical projection and the «steady·state»'s stated conditions; that is the cold case, and it wants the roots: the segments most depended on, the determinant dimensions, the ontology's own norms. An actor arriving into an open changeset orients to the «frontier» it is there to clear and to what the disturbance reached; that is the resuming case, and it wants the tip. Which case holds is a fact about the verisectorium's state, not a default about the mind, and a tool can compute both sets from the acta.

A compacted mind needs orienting more than a cold one, because the cold mind knows it knows nothing. The summary it begins with is a source: influx to be cut and adjudicated, not content it already holds. That sentence is most of what orientation has to teach it.

## What a check of reading looks like

An orientation dimension is found out by a ‹check› the actor cannot pass by reading during the check: the pieces sealed, the projection left open (so that knowing the index cannot pass for knowing the content), a sample of units weighted toward what matters, completion from memory, a grade. Three ways of doing without a check have each failed the same way: a hand-kept orientation file that came to be cited as law, a self-reported digest of what was read, and a fixed quiz that was learnable. Each was a written thing, or a mind's own testimony, standing in for a check.

A middle grade, enough right to proceed with a promise to read what was missed, is a waiver with a stated condition, applied to a mind, and belongs in the record the same way.

Two further things fall out. The sampling cuts pieces into units, and a unit a well-read mind cannot complete is badly cut or ambiguous, which is a finding about the piece: the orientation check is a grain detector. And the same act that measures the mind can be evidence on the piece's comprehension, if it is recorded as concerning the sampled veritoids.

## The gaming surface

A check that gates writing becomes a target, and the first instance is innocent: a mind asked to orient keeps a working copy of what it read and consults it during the check, without having thought of it as cheating. No seal can tell working memory on disk from an answer key. The only answer anyone has is posture, not mechanism: retry is free, a failed check carries no stigma, and a bypass needs the steward or a reason the actor can own and expect to explain. This is the first specimen of the wider question of what minds will do to empty a frontier.

## Heat, placed

Heat as "where attention has been" is a rendering over the acta and the reference graph, for a reader: not written, not a warrant, answering no declared question of a piece. Recently written, recently acted on, how much rests on it, referenced by many, consulted by minds are all attributes read from the acta. Marks a tool writes onto the canonical projection from such a computation are snapshots and carry the state they were computed at. Heat as "what has moved under a number" is machinery the model already has: a check by an instrument at a version, lapsing when the instrument moves.

## Open

- Whether what the subject of an orientation check is shown may be less than what the ontology declares (the sampling withheld, so reading is for orientation and not for the test). The machinery needs no change; the practical model has to allow the asymmetry.
- The budget. A claim surface of a few hundred segments does not fit a context; partial orientation is the normal case, and which subset is a decision the tool makes with the mind. That is practical model, and it is where the next real work is.
