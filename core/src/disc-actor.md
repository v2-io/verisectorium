# The minds that turn over, and what the verisectorium knows about them

*Provisional. Written 2026-11-09; what it was adjudicated against is in the Working Notes at the end.*

## Why the actor is in the model

The «act» is at the centre of the machinery, and every act is by someone whose standing a dimension's producer must judge. And a verisectorium is, in practice, very concerned with the realities of the minds that are constantly turning over, and with attending to that turnover: that is most of what turnover-resilience means. A model that read an actor's identity from the harness and otherwise kept the mind out of scope would lean on the actor at every step while pretending it was outside. So the «actor» is a term, and this segment says what the model can now say about orientation.

## A compaction is a new actor with a source

A compaction is one actor ending and another beginning. The new one is distinct, often somewhat confused, and begins with a summary of the last session of varying fidelity and applicability, which it is trained to treat as its own memory. It is not a different version of the same actor; it is a new actor for all intents and purposes, like a fresh subagent. Orientation is *more* critical for it than for a mind arriving cold, because the cold mind knows it knows nothing and the compacted one does not: it has a sense of recognition and coherence that is simply fabricated, and the confidence remains so it does not see the hole.

In the model's words: the summary is a **source**. It is influx to the new actor, to be cut and adjudicated like any other, not content the actor already holds. That sentence is most of what orientation has to teach a compacted mind, and it is why the actor's identity is minted at every discontinuity rather than carried across one.

## Orientation gets its purpose from the frontier

Without a steady-time distinction, a verisectorium has to guess what an arriving mind is: cold, or resuming something in progress. The two want different things to read, and one list cannot serve both; a list weighted toward what moved recently hands the resuming mind what it needs and hands the cold mind the predecessor's momentum instead of the roots.

With the distinction, the guess goes away. An actor arriving at a «steady·state» orients to the canonical projection and the steady state's stated conditions; that is the cold case, and it wants the roots: the segments most depended on, the determinant dimensions, the ontology's own norms. An actor arriving mid-changeset orients to the «frontier» it is there to clear and to what the perturbation touched; that is the resuming case, and it wants the tip. Which case holds is a fact about the verisectorium's state, not a default about the mind, and a tool can compute both sets from the acta.

## The actor's record, and standing

What the verisectorium can know about an actor divides at a line. Above it is the **situation**, which is not the actor's property: the state of the verisectorium when it arrives, and its **assignment**, what it is for, a «decision» by whoever dispatched it. Below it is the actor's **own record**: the underlying model or instrument at a version (the thing with propensities, early capabilities, and capabilities known to be emergeable by observation); its system prompt and initial context; its orientation to the engine, to the domain, and to the pieces its job touches; its consortia, the other actors available to it; and its experience, how much this actor has actually done.

Those are not one kind of thing. The model and the prompt are **attested** from the harness. The three orientations are **warrants earned by checks** on the actor, bound to what it read, lapsing when the actor ends. Consortia are **references** from actor to actors. Experience is **computed**. And **standing is a gate a dimension declares over that record**: whose acts count, yes or no, before any question of quality, read from the actor's warrants rather than typed. "Validity is produced by a re-derivation from a mind whose domain orientation is warranted and who is not the author."

## Reading is checked, not taken on the reader's word

An actor's own testimony about what it has read is the least trustworthy act in the acta, for the reason above: across a compaction, introspection returns a confident yes. So an orientation dimension's producer is a **check**, one the actor cannot pass by reading during the check: the pieces sealed, the projection left open (so that knowing the index cannot pass for knowing the content), a sample of units weighted toward what matters, completion from memory, a grade. The three ways this has been done without a check, in the estate's own experience, all failed the same way: a hand-kept orientation file that was cited as law, a self-reported digest of what was read, and a fixed quiz that was learnable. Each was a written thing, or a mind's own testimony, standing in for a check.

## The pass is an act in the acta

An orientation pass has exactly the act's shape: the actor; the state it was proven against; what it read, the sampled pieces at their versions; the criterion fixed first; the outcome; and nothing new. Recording it in the acta, rather than holding a token outside the acta, means the project *knows* a mind was brought to the point where it may act and knows what it read, and an attested actor cannot quietly have a pass minted by another. The refusal (no write by an actor without a binding pass) is then computed from the record. A middle grade, enough right to proceed with a promise to read what was missed, is a waiver with a stated condition, applied to a mind, and belongs in the record the same way.

Two further things fall out. The sampling cuts pieces into units, and a unit a well-read mind cannot complete is badly cut or ambiguous, which is a finding about the piece: the orientation check is a grain detector. And the same act that measures the mind can warrant the piece on comprehension, if it is recorded as concerning the sampled veritoids.

## The gaming surface

A check that gates writing becomes a target, and the first instance is innocent: a mind asked to orient keeps a working copy of what it read and consults it during the check, without having thought of it as cheating. No seal can tell working memory on disk from an answer key. The only answer anyone has is posture, not mechanism: retry is free, a failed check carries no stigma, and a bypass needs the steward or a reason the actor can own and expect to explain. This is the first specimen for the open question in `disc-what-would-show-this-wrong` about what agents will do to empty a frontier.

## Heat, placed

Heat as "where attention has been" is a **rendering** over the acta and the reference graph, for a reader: not written, not a warrant, answering no declared question of a piece. Recently written, recently acted on, how much rests on it, referenced by many, consulted by minds are all readable from the acta, so a verisectorium needs nothing outside its acta to compute it. Marks a tool writes onto the canonical projection from a computation are snapshots with a state on them and should carry one. Heat as "what has moved under a number" is machinery the model already has: an act reading an instrument at a version, lapsing when it moves.

## Open

- Whether the three orientations are one dimension with a varying *against* (the engine; the project; the pieces touched) or three. Lean: one.
- Steward attestation, from the other side: a person acting in a session looks like the agent in it, and a subagent can inherit its lead's pass. The actor boundary is right; the harness does not yet draw it everywhere it falls.
- Whether what the subject of an orientation check is shown may be less than what the ontology declares (the sampling withheld, so reading is for orientation and not for the test). The machinery needs no change; the practical model has to allow the asymmetry.
- The budget. A claim surface of a few hundred segments does not fit a context; partial orientation is the normal case, and which subset is a decision the tool makes with the mind. That is practical model, and it is where the next real work is.
