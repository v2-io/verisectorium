# An audit comes in

*An invented example. It illustrates the terms; it is evidence for nothing.*

**What this shows.** Taking material in is the first thing a «verisectorium» does, and it needs no machinery of its own: a source is an assembly, its chunks are veritoids, where each chunk goes is a «decision», and the inbox and retirement are computed. One small audit is cut, routed five ways and retired. Running it found that the cut itself has to be revisable, and three other places where the model has to choose.

## The material

A result T, accepted: a claim c, an imports list i that cites Smith's Lemma 3 for one step, and an explanation e. An outline O that points at T.

An old audit of T arrives, five bullets of prose by an auditor nobody can now ask:

1. "The bound in c is loose; I believe a tighter one holds."
2. "c fails at n = 0."
3. "The second sentence of e is unclear."
4. "The key step is Smith's Lemma 3. ✓ The derivation is sound."
5. "The notation for norms is inconsistent across the whole volume."

## The run

| step | what happens | what kind of thing it is | afterwards |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A mind brings the audit in and cuts it: five chunks, one per bullet, each with a bears-on reference (1, 2, 4 to c; 3 to e; 5 to O). An instrument confirms the chunks cover the whole text. | a new ‹source› segment; where to cut and what each chunk bears on are the cutter's decisions | Nothing in canon is disturbed and nothing is owed. T's inbox shows chunks 1 to 4; O's shows chunk 5. The source is 0 of 5 routed. |
| 2 | A router R takes chunk 2. It is a claim that something in canon is wrong, from an auditor no harness attests. R records its own ‹testimony› that the audit says so, as a finding relayed against c, and routes the chunk: *recorded as a finding*. | a relayed finding; a routing decision | A changeset opens on T with a question **raised** on c: does it fail at n = 0? c is not unsettled; a check with standing will bear the finding out or not. What becomes of it is no longer the chunk's business. 1 of 5. |
| 3 | Chunk 3, likewise: a relayed finding against e. | a relayed finding; a routing decision | **raised**: e, in the same changeset. 2 of 5. |
| 4 | Chunk 4 says two things, so R splits it. 4a, "the key step is Smith's Lemma 3": *already present*, in i, and the decision's basis is i at its version. 4b, "✓ the derivation is sound": a confirmation by a mind no harness attests, by an unknown means. R records it as testimony that the audit said so, which is no line of evidence for c, and routes it: *recorded as testimony*. | a recut of the source; two routing decisions; a testimony | 4 of 6. |
| 5 | Chunk 1 is an idea, not a defect. T's author writes a provisional claim c′ into a new slot of T ("a tighter bound, unproven") and R routes the chunk: *became a new piece*, basis c′. | a write to T; a routing decision | c′ is provisional and owes nothing. It is where the idea now lives. 5 of 6. |
| 6 | Chunk 5 is not about T or O. R routes it: *delivered to* the notation verisectorium, where it arrives as influx. | a routing decision | 6 of 6. **The source is retired**, computed. T's and O's inboxes are empty. The changeset opened at step 2 is still open: two questions wait for checks, and T waits for a whole read. |
| 7 | Weeks later T's author trims the imports list and drops the citation of Smith. | a write to T | The routing of 4a rested on i as it was: **reopened**. Chunk 4a is back in T's inbox and the source is no longer retired. Nothing was lost by the trim without someone being told. |

## What this says about the model

- **No machinery of its own.** Every step was a segment written, a decision recorded or something computed.
- **"Found everything" and "found something" are told apart**, because the cut covers the whole source and the count is of chunks routed for good, not of things noticed.
- **A source can be retired, and can come back.** Retirement is computed from routing decisions that stand. Step 7 is the case that matters: material delivered into a piece is not lost when the piece is later rewritten.
- **Evidence that arrives as prose enters as what it is.** A defect reported by an unattested auditor raises a question on the router's testimony and unsettles nothing; it earns its weight when a check with standing bears it out. A confirmation reported the same way counts for nothing, and its criterion is worth keeping as a check to run.
- **An idea has a cheap home.** Chunk 1 became a provisional piece in one write, and the piece itself owes nothing. The write did change T's slots, so T's whole read has lapsed and T's acceptance is reopened until someone reads it whole again.

**Where it has to choose, and has not:**

1. **The cut must be revisable.** Step 4 split a chunk and could as easily have re-pointed one. A source's text is never rewritten; its cut and what each chunk bears on are discovered in the routing, like any grain.
2. **What a routing decision reads.** Run here as the target at its version, which is what made step 7 work. It also means every rewording of a piece reopens every routing into it unless a carrying check says the edit kept what was delivered. For a piece with thirty chunks routed into it that is thirty judgments per edit, or one judgment covering thirty, which is a stamp. Lean: as run, for the outcomes that claim the corpus now holds something (*already present*, *changed an existing piece*, *became a new piece*); and the edit's author is asked once, per changeset, with the routed chunks shown.
3. **Who may route.** Recording a finding should be open to anyone. Setting a chunk aside or rejecting it is where material is lost. Lean: rejection needs a mind other than the cutter, and its reason.
4. **Whether bringing a source in opens a changeset.** Run here as not: nothing in canon was disturbed until step 2. So a verisectorium can be steady with a thousand unrouted chunks, and the pile is visible only as a count. Whether some dimension of the whole should hold that count to anything is each verisectorium's to say.

**What it did not exercise.** A chunk routed as *changed an existing piece*; a rejection; one more independent arrival of something already held (evidence that two sources say it, not a duplicate); a source too large to cut in one sitting.

## Historical Context
What sat in the Working Notes of the estate's theory files was three things: inbox chunks; standing content that belongs in slots of its own kind (regression guards, open questions with closers, dead ends with reasons); and in-place fate markers written as prose ("Resolved 2026-10-30 in …"), which are routing decisions that never became acts. In the one sprint that measured it, no source was ever retired and every pass left more surface than it removed.
