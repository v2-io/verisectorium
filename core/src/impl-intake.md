# Intake needs no machinery of its own

Taking material in is the first thing a verisectorium does, and it needs no machinery beyond the defs. A **source** is a ‹source›: an assembly holding **chunks**, veritoids of a chunk kind, and the cut must cover the whole source so that "every chunk dealt with" cannot mean "found something". A chunk carries a *bears-on* reference to the segment it concerns. Where a chunk goes is a «decision», answering the dimension *where did it go*: it changed an existing piece, became a new piece, was already present, is one more independent arrival (evidence, not a duplicate), was superseded or rejected with a reason, was delivered to another verisectorium, was recorded as a finding against a piece, or was passed to a segment's inbox, which is not final. A segment's **inbox** is computed: the chunks bearing on it with no final routing. The chunk lives in its source, so adding to or draining an inbox never changes the segment; that is the measured lesson of the sprint. Who has read a source, and how far, is found out about the source; a reader's own word for it is testimony, the weakest evidence there is. A source is retired when every chunk has a final routing, computed.

Much influx is evidence that arrives as prose: an old audit saying "this claim is false", or "the bound holds ✓". Its author is not an attested actor, and nothing typed can make it one. So a chunk that says something is wrong is routed into the acta as a ‹finding› against the piece, recorded by the router on the router's ‹testimony› that the source says so, and it is owed like any finding until answered. A chunk that says something is right counts for nothing until someone re-runs it; what is worth carrying from it is its criterion, as a check owed.

What sat in ASF's Working Notes was three things: inbox chunks; standing content that belongs in slots of its own kind (regression guards, open questions with closers, dead ends with reasons); and in-place fate markers written as prose ("Resolved 2026-10-30 in …"), which are routing decisions that never became acts.
