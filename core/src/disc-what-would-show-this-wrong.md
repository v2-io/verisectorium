# What would show this wrong, and what I expect to strain

**The practical test.** Three things the October sprint on ASF showed, that the model has to make impossible or at least visible. (1) Nobody could see which influx had been looked at, by whom, or how far; the sprint could not tell "found everything" from "found something". (2) Checks and fixes fed each other; almost half of the claims "fixed" had only been written earlier in the same sprint. (3) No source was ever retired; every pass left more surface than it removed. The model answers (1) with sources cut completely and a routing decision for every chunk, and (3) with the inbox outside the segment and computed retirement; (2) it only permits an answer to (a checker's findings go back to the piece's author; see «changeset»). A first real intake is judged by these three.

The model is wrong if a real verisectorium needs a thing that is neither written, done nor computed; if something it adjudicates is neither a truth claim standing by evidence nor a «decision» standing by backing, nor built from those; if a warrant cannot be read from acts without someone typing it after all; or if the terms have to multiply back toward eighty to do real work.

Before any more material, my guess at what one real root will strain, in order:

1. **The cost of recording.** Per-piece answers, and per-act read sets and means, on 1,234 records. If an act costs more than a sentence of prose, agents will write prose, and the estate's dead `stage` fields say this decides everything.
2. **What a derivation step reads.** Honest reads include the premises; then every premise change lapses every dependent's checks and reopens every acceptance that rested on them, which is right and expensive. A verisectorium may want *reads* to distinguish "relied on at a warrant" from "read the text".
3. **What an acceptance reads.** As written, rewording an accepted piece reopens its acceptance and that of everything accepted on it. If small fixes stop being made, or re-accepting becomes a stamp, the basis is drawn wrongly.
4. **The steward's decisions.** "Only Joseph may rule" has no mechanism while his session and an agent in it look alike to the harness.
5. **Whether *against* carries the second argument everywhere**, or whether importance-for-a-target and comprehension-by-a-reader need the target as content of the act rather than of the dimension.
6. **Waivers and affirmations as cheap exits.** Any marker of done becomes a target; both are decisions with a party and a reason, visible at every steady state, and that is all the model offers against gaming.
7. **Independence.** Evidence adds only along independent lines, and nearly every actor is an instance of one model family. Different means can be required; a different failure mode cannot yet.

Two things the first cold read found are still gaps in the outline: how an author names a held segment at write time, when the model is finer than anything authors write or tools record today; and where a change to the ontology is recorded.
