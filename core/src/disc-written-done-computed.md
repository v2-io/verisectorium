# Written, done, computed

| | What | Who sets it | How it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **written** | a veritoid's content and references; an assembly's slots, their states and what fills each; a segment's history, where its kind has a slot for it | an author | only by a segment changeset |
| **done** | acts. Finding out: who checked, found or testified what, against which versions, answering which dimension, by what means, with what outcome. Making so: who decided what, about what, on what basis | an actor, at a moment | appended, never edited; evidence is overturned by later evidence, a decision is changed only by its party |
| **computed** | every warrant; whether each reference holds; whether each decision still stands; the frontier; whether a state is a steady state; every attribute and roll-up; which chunks are in each segment's inbox | the verisectorium, as of a state | by recomputation, never by anyone |

Both kinds of statement cross the table the same way. A truth claim is written, the evidence for it is done, and how well it stands is computed. A choice is written, the «decision» is done, and whether it still stands is computed.

Which chunks are in an inbox is computed, but what is in it is influx: outside the segment's written state, with no warrant, drained rather than kept. Acts are the input both sides answer to. A typed status word is a written thing wearing computed clothes; the whole discipline is the line in this table.
