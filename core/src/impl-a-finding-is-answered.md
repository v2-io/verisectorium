# A rewording, a wrong finding and a right one

*An invented example. It illustrates the terms; it is evidence for nothing.*

**What this shows.** Three «acts|def/act» that concern an earlier act do the work that would otherwise fall to re-doing everything or to waivers. A rewording keeps its checks by being *carried*. A finding that is wrong is *contested* and stops counting without the piece being touched. A finding that is right is *answered* by someone other than the author of the repair. Running it found four places where the model has to choose.

## The material

The ontology's rule for canon: a claim is accepted while a mind outside its author's lineage has re-derived it and no finding or contradiction stands against it unanswered; a document is accepted while its claims are, nothing unanswered stands against anything it holds, and someone outside its author's lineage has read it whole.

Steady state S0. T is a result written by A. It holds a claim c, which carries a hypothesis H (n is at least 1), a derivation d, and an explanation e that depends on c. B re-derived c by way of d. G read T whole. T and c are accepted.

## The run

| step | what happens | the frontier afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A rewords c: the same statement in a clearer order. One changeset on T; d and e carried with A's affirmation. | B's re-derivation read the old c: lapsed, so c's acceptance is **reopened**. G's whole read lapsed: T's acceptance is **reopened**. |
| 2 | C, who is not of A's lineage, sets the two versions of c beside B's criterion and records that the edit left unchanged what B's check relied on: *equivalent*. C does the same for G's whole read. | B's check and G's read are carried and bind at the new version. The rule accepts c and T. Empty. **S1**, at the cost of two judgments on a diff. |
| 3 | An auditor D records a ‹finding›: "c fails at n = 0." | **found**: c. An unanswered finding stands against c, so the rule no longer accepts it: c and T are **reopened**. |
| 4 | A thinks the finding is wrong, since H excludes n = 0. A cannot clear it: there is no repair to answer, and A is the author. E, of neither A's lineage nor D's, checks the finding itself, criterion fixed first ("does c as written assert anything at n = 0?"), and records that it does not. E also records a finding of its own: e never mentions H, and a reader takes c to cover n = 0. | D's finding is overturned and stops counting; c is accepted again, untouched. **found**: e, by E. T stays **reopened**. |
| 5 | A rewrites e to state H. One changeset on T; c and d untouched. | G's whole read, which read e, has lapsed. E's finding does not lapse with the text it read. |
| 6 | D reads the new e against E's finding and records it *answered*. G reads T whole again; nobody offers to carry that read, since e changed in substance. | Empty. **S2.** |

## What this says about the model

- **Carrying is how a small edit stays small.** Without it, step 1 costs a re-derivation and a whole re-read, and nobody tidies an accepted piece. With it the cost is a second mind's judgment that the edit changed nothing the earlier act relied on. The carried check is still B's one line of evidence; carrying adds no line, and it adds a dependency: contest C's judgment and B's check lapses again.
- **A wrong finding has a way out that leaves the piece alone.** Before acts could concern acts, a mistaken contradiction could be cleared only by rewriting the piece or by a waiver. Here it was contested, by a mind with no stake in either side, and the piece was never touched.
- **A wrong finding still did work.** E's check of D's finding is what found the real defect, in the explanation. The record keeps both.
- **The author cleared nothing.** A wrote one repair and affirmed two pieces. Every item was cleared by someone else's act. Independence sits on the checking side.
- **Bad news outlasted an edit.** At step 5 the rewrite did not clear E's finding; D's answer at step 6 did.

**Where it has to choose, and has not:**

1. **Whether a finding unsettles at once.** Run here as it does: one sentence from an auditor reopened two acceptances at step 3, and the finding was wrong. That is cheap to do and costly to undo. The alternative is that a finding only shows, beside the piece, until a check with a criterion confirms it. Lean: at once, with who may record a finding that counts declared by the dimension, since the cost of the other error is a known defect nobody is told about.
2. **What ends a disagreement between checks.** E overturned D. D could contest E. Lean: a contest of a contest is not read by rule; it is routed to a decider, which is what "every adjudication ends in a decision" says.
3. **How far a check may be carried.** Carry upon carry across many small edits can end far from what B re-derived. Lean: the dimension's *read as* may cap it (re-derive after so many carries, or when the carried text has drifted past some measure).
4. **Partial answers.** "c fails at n = 0, and e is unclear" is two findings in one sentence; answering half of it has no outcome. Lean: a finding answered in part is split, as a veritoid is when a check confirms part of it.

## Working Notes
- From the one real record read through this (a scope segment's 162 acts): eight rewording judgments, all by a mind other than the editor, two of them *not equivalent*; thirty-three answerings, eighteen by someone who wrote no repair; no contest that overturned a check.
