# What "better" is

A «steady·state» is *feasible*: every constraint holds or is waived. **Better** is a direction within feasibility: a later steady state is better than an earlier one when, on the determinant dimensions, no warrant has fallen and some has risen, or the content answers to a determinant more strongly in a sense the ontology declares (AAT: a claim that entails another is stronger; weaker hypotheses, stronger conclusion). Determinants do not weigh equally, and the weight is not a property of the determinant alone: it depends on the kind of segment it is asked of and on the purpose asking. In a theory, factual correctness under stated conditions, fruitfulness and novelty of the primary claims outweigh notational coherence by a great deal; carve a subgraph off to become a paper and citation fidelity and the novelty claim rise to the top for that subgraph. So the weighting is declared where the purpose is, on a projection, over segment kind × determinant, with reach (how much in that projection rests on the piece) as a multiplier, and the canonical projection's declaration is the default. Unweighted, two steady states can each be better on a different determinant with neither better overall, and choosing is a decision; weighted by a purpose, the purpose may compute a number over the whole. No composite score lives on a piece. Where effort goes next is a «decision» either way. The one place the estate combines scores openly is when choosing between candidates in a decision, and that is where it should stay.

This is why softening is honest and still not enough: lowering an assertion is the cheapest way back to feasibility, and a verisectorium softened to tautologies would be perfectly steady and worth nothing. Strengthen-first is what the direction adds and the engine does not enforce.

The two regimes need nothing of their own. A theory claim asserts flatly and its determinant dimensions read true or false; its doubt is a list of named open checks beside it, not a number. An empirical fact or a proposed model is read by degree. They join at the hypothesis: a flat if-then resting on a model whose adequacy carries the graded part. A verisectorium that wants numeric credence declares a dimension whose *read as* computes one from the acts, as relata does.
