# What "better" is

A «steady·state» is *feasible*: every constraint holds or is waived. **Better** is a direction within feasibility: both states are read from the record as it stands at the later one, so that an earlier state is judged by what is now known of it. In that reading a later state is better when, on the determinant dimensions, in this order: less of what canon asserts is known to be wrong; no warrant has fallen that was not shown to have been unearned, and some has risen; or content that stands equally well says more (in a theory: a claim that entails another is stronger; weaker hypotheses, stronger conclusion). So catching a false claim and replacing it with a narrower true one is an improvement, though the new text claims less and its warrant reads the same as the old one once did: read from the later record, the old claim was contradicted. Determinants do not weigh equally, and the weight is not a property of the determinant alone: it depends on the kind of segment it is asked of and on the purpose asking. In a theory, factual correctness under stated conditions, fruitfulness and novelty of the primary claims outweigh notational coherence by a great deal; carve a subgraph off to become a paper and citation fidelity and the novelty claim rise to the top for that subgraph. So the weighting is declared where the purpose is, on a projection, over segment kind × determinant, with reach (how much in that projection rests on the piece) as a multiplier, and the canonical projection's declaration is the default. Unweighted, two steady states can each be better on a different determinant with neither better overall, and choosing is a decision; weighted by a purpose, the purpose may compute a number over the whole. No composite score lives on a piece. Where effort goes next is a «decision» either way. The one place the estate combines scores openly is when choosing between candidates in a decision, and that is where it should stay.

Narrowing a claim that has been contradicted is a repair. Narrowing a claim that has only been questioned, to be rid of the question, is softening: the cheapest way back to feasibility, honest, and by the third clause a loss. A verisectorium softened to tautologies would be perfectly steady and worth nothing. Strengthen-first is what the direction adds and the engine does not enforce: before narrowing, try to make the stronger claim stand, and where it cannot, the reason it cannot is itself a result. Which was done, and why, is a «decision» on record.

The two regimes need nothing of their own. A theory claim asserts flatly and its determinant dimensions read true or false; its doubt is a list of named open checks beside it, not a number. An empirical fact or a proposed model is read by degree. They join at the hypothesis: a flat if-then resting on a model whose adequacy carries the graded part. A verisectorium that wants numeric credence declares a dimension whose *read as* computes one from the acts, as the estate's citation store does.
