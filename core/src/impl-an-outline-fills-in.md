# An outline fills in

*An invented example. It runs on a reading of the model that the defs do not yet state: a segment is a veritoid or an assembly of slots, and suggested and promised are written states of a slot. It illustrates that reading and shows where it cannot yet answer; it is evidence for nothing.*

**What this shows.** Whether a thing is wanted in a place, and whether the thing is accepted, are kept apart at every step without strain: the first moves by writes to the segment that has the slot, the second by acts on whatever fills it. Working it through found six places where the model has to choose and has not.

## The material

An outline O, which is canonical. It is a segment whose slots are an ordered list:

1. filled, holding a veritoid D directly: a one-line definition. D is accepted.
2. filled, pointing at a segment R1: a result, with a claim c1 that is predicated on D and an explanation. R1 is accepted.
3. **suggested**, empty: "a result about X is probably wanted here", left by an adviser who is not working on this.
4. a **gap**: "something is missing between this and whatever follows; not known what".

A reading path P, not canonical, with two slots: one pointing at R1, one pointing at D.

D is *held* by O: it has no name of its own and changes only when O is written. R1 is *pointed at*: it is written on its own. At S0 every reference holds and nothing is owed. The suggested slot and the gap are there, and S0 is still a steady state.

## The run

| step | what happens | what kind of thing it is | the frontier afterwards |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Agent A, at work on what follows R1, sees that a result R2 is needed and says so: slot 3 goes from suggested to **promised**, with the kind and name it will have. | a write to O | Slot 3 is promised and empty: **owed**. The last whole read of O read its slot list, which changed: lapsed. |
| 2 | A writes R2 (a claim c2, predicated on c1 and on D, asserting exact; an explanation) and fills slot 3 by pointing at it. | a new segment; a write to O | The slot is filled. R2 is not accepted, so nothing it says inside itself is owed. Whether the promise is now discharged is the first open point below. |
| 3 | B, who did not write it, re-derives c2. | an act on c2 | c2 has a warrant. It is still not accepted; a piece is checked before it is accepted, not after. |
| 4 | The steward and the lead accept R2 together. | an act on R2's pieces, by those with standing to accept | R2 is canon. Now the constraints apply to it: c2 asserts exact and is warranted re-derived; what it rests on (c1, D) is accepted. Owed: a whole read of O. |
| 5 | A rewords D. | a write to O, since O holds D | The acceptance of D read the old D and no longer binds: **D is not accepted**. c1 and c2 are accepted pieces resting on a piece that is not: both **unsettled**. B's re-derivation of c2 read D: lapsed, so c2 is an **overclaim**. Their references to D are stale. P's slot pointing at D is stale too. |
| 6 | The steward and the lead accept the new D. B re-derives c2. Someone other than its author re-reads c1 against the new D and confirms it. The references are reconciled. O is read whole. | acts; two small writes | Empty. **S1.** |

At S1 the gap in slot 4 is still a gap. It is shown with the steady state as a known truth about what is missing, and it owes nothing.

## What this says about the model

**It held.** At no step did "is it wanted here" and "is it accepted" need to be the same question. Suggested to promised to filled was three writes to O. Unadjudicated to checked to accepted was two acts on R2. A reader of O at any step could tell the adviser's idea (slot 3 before step 1) from the thing that must be written (slot 3 after it), which is what a mind arriving cold needs.

**Where it has to choose, and has not:**

1. **What discharges a promise.** After step 2 the promised slot holds a draft nobody has accepted. If writing discharges the promise, any draft does. If only acceptance does, the slot stays owed through step 3. Lean: only acceptance.
2. **What acceptance binds to.** Run as an ordinary act, the acceptance of D lapsed the moment D was reworded (step 5), and everything accepted that rests on D became unsettled. That is the "big perturbation" in small, and it means an accepted piece cannot be touched up without being put up again. Lean: that is right, and worth saying out loud, since the alternative is an accepted mark that survives changes to what was accepted.
3. **What an assembly's version covers.** For O's whole read to lapse at step 1 (its slot list changed) and not at step 3 or 4 (nothing in O changed), an assembly's version has to be its own slots, their states, and which piece fills each, and not the versions of the pieces filling them. Otherwise every edit anywhere changes every outline above it. The defs say the opposite today.
4. **Held or pointed at.** The example needed both, and the difference that did the work was one thing only: a held piece (D) is written with its parent, and a pointed-at piece (R1, R2) is written on its own. If that is the whole difference, "one kind of thing nested to any depth" needs nothing else: a slot is filled by holding or by pointing, and the unit of writing is a piece together with everything it holds.
5. **A reading path that goes stale.** At step 5, P's slot pointing at D is stale. P is not canon and asserts nothing about D. Lean: shown to whoever reads P, owed by nobody.
6. **Who may promise, and who may accept.** Step 1 says A may promise because A is in the work; step 4 says only the steward and the lead accept. Both are standing, and neither is declared anywhere yet.

**What it did not exercise.** The inbox, which is the other main place a segment says what it wants and intends: here every intention arrived already shaped as a slot, where in practice most arrive as a note beside the segment, and the adviser's vague idea at S0 was more honestly a note on O than a suggested slot. A piece three levels deep by holding (a derivation held inside a result, with steps held inside it); a slot whose kind fixes it, such as a result's required claim, being promised; a gap being resolved into slots.

## Working Notes
- The six points above are the decisions this move puts to «segment», «veritoid», «kind», «reference» and «act» together. None is written into a def.
- Open. Whether a suggested slot and a gap should appear in a steady state's stated conditions or only be counted. The run shows the gap and says nothing of the suggestion.
