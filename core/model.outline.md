# Verisectorium Model

The canonical projection of `core/`: everything the model consists of, in a considered order (mostly dependence; where a word is used before its row, the row is near), with what is settled and what is not marked on every row. Intro, discussion and implication segments (`src/`) are interspersed where they belong in the reading; each names in its frontmatter the defs it narrates, so a change to a def says which prose must be re-read. Read the rows in order.

> [!LEGEND]
> - `mark`: this outline is an assembly, and the mark is what each slot and its filler stand at. **accepted** (what fills the slot is in canon: decided by steward and lead, in unity), **promised** (the slot is promised and nothing accepted fills it yet), **provisional** (not accepted; it claims only that the suggestion exists; the `def` column says whether anything fills the slot yet), **gap** (something is known missing; what replaces it is not known).
> - `def`: `present` (a full def file), `stub`, `inline` (the description is the whole definition; no file), `–` (none yet).
> - `term`: a link to `def/<term>.md`; for an inline row the plain word; for a `src/` segment a link to `src/<slug>.md`, with its form (`intro`, `disc`, `impl`) in the `def` column. Terms are nouns in the singular; a multi-word term joins its words with `·`. In prose a term is written `«term»`; a subtype `‹subtype›`; inline words are written plainly.
> - `subtypes`: declared in the term's def; they have no file of their own.
>
> `bin/defs check core` verifies that every «term» and ‹subtype› resolves, that files and rows agree, and that relations point at terms.

## Working Notes
- The first thing for Joseph is not on the list below: «verisectorium»'s Working Notes open with the one question the second real root could not get past (what, in asf, is canon).
- For Joseph's vote, since the files cannot show it: what the lead chose on 2026-11-10, in the bodies, on questions that had been parked for him or were new. (1) The two atoms as the root, with «decision» a term. (2) A version covers normalized content and reference targets; an assembly's version covers its slots and not its fillers. (3) The version a reference last saw is read from the acta, and reconciling is an affirmation, not a write. (4) What content says about itself is what is claimed; it is not compared with a warrant, and what gates canon is the acceptance decision or rule. (5) Only canon owes; a reopened acceptance leaves a piece in canon, unsettled. (6) Acyclicity belongs to reference kinds a dimension caps along. (7) Means are declared by the dimension, and lines of evidence are counted by shared source and lineage. (8) A finding or contradiction outlasts an edit until answered by a mind other than the repairer; a carrying check lets earlier acts bind across an edit judged equivalent. (9) Author and party are seats; independence is read through what an actor inherited (a summary, a context, a brief), so a bare-briefed dispatch is independent and a fork is not. (10) "Acceptance" means admitting to canon; leaving an owed item owed is a waiver. (12) Better is read from the later state's record, with correction ranked first, so that repairing a false claim counts as an improvement. (13) A decision that needs several seats is made of their assents, one act each. (14) A whole does not rest on its parts: an assembly is accepted on a read of the whole and is not unsettled by an unsettled part. (15) A decision about the subject has a record that is content (wording, alternatives, grounds as references, what would reopen it), and the deciding act reads that record; grounds, assumptions and observations worth naming are written as pieces. (16) A finding relayed from an unattested source raises a question and unsettles nothing. (11) Steadiness is said of a place: an atomic changeset has a computed scope, several may be open, and the verisectorium is steady outside them; this takes up the question of parallel changesets that Joseph had punted, because without it waivers become the ordinary way to close. Each is argued in the def it sits in; none has been tested on a real root.
- The vocabulary. Counting everything named (terms, subtypes, inline rows, frontier items, slot states, reference kinds, the acts about acts) the model is at about eighty-five, which is the count the falsifier in `disc-what-would-show-this-wrong` names. The first core's eighty-five were terms defined against one another before any example was run; these were each introduced by a move that needed them. That difference is real and is not what the falsifier says. The better test is whether a cold mind can build an instance from the defs alone. One has (`msc/scratch/instance-trial-1/`, a small specification, by an Opus agent that read only core): it used about sixty of the words, never needed a dozen of them, and was short of relations, not words.
- "History" is used in three senses (the line of steady states; prose kept with a segment; version control of renderings). Lean: keep the second, and say "the steady line" and "the renderings' history" for the others.
- Size: `bin/nutshell check` prints the nutshell's size; nothing yet prints the size of what a reader must read to hold the whole model, which is some ten times that. `disc-actor` and «actor» say much the same thing twice.
- The outline's gap rows are open questions, which the model would cut as veritoids of a question kind, not gaps in its own sense (a slot whose kind is unknown).
- Run one more real root, from a different volume, before adding any term.
- Which of the inline rows wants to be a term? The rule: it becomes one when its Working Notes would hold a decision nobody else's file can hold.
- From the second trial (`msc/scratch/instance-trial-2/`, a real TST derivation, by a Fable agent that read only core), not yet taken up: a cap across kinds beyond the one sentence now in «dimension»; work owed by someone (asf's 632 owned items) beyond a raised question assigned to a seat; a check that concerns a rendering; one act that asked two dimensions. What it showed that the tool and the file do not: the claim in the segment's title has never been the subject of any check, and five checks the tool lapsed still bind.
- From the trial instance, not yet taken up: a way for an instrument's means to be told from a different dimension when it is held against something other than the dimension's own *against*; non-determinant dimensions never asked appearing nowhere in a steady state's conditions.
- Still untested by any move: a delivery (a paper's projection with its own weighting, and a rendering sent out and pinned at a state). An intake has now been run. The purpose leads the definition, and delivery is now its least-exercised half.

- On size and register. The whole model was once explained top to bottom, each word introduced before use, with one running example and one picture, in about three hundred and fifty lines. This core should end no larger than that in what a reader must read to hold it, and simpler; it is not there yet (it has no single picture).

## Historical Context
This outline does not descend from the first core (`msc/notes/17b-2026-11-07-first-core/`, eighty-five rows). Its terms were earned again from the notes and from two worked runs, and a word from the first core is here only where the model needed it. Nothing was left out as a class: an idea from the first core, or from the kernel spikes that fed its stubs, is in or out on whether the model is better with it, and the ones still being weighed are in the Working Notes of the def they concern. The claim shape for a theory (context, hypotheses, conclusion, derivation, strength, sharpness, forced) and the low-fidelity kinds are one verisectorium's choices and belong in AAT's ontology.
