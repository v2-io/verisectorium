# «verisectorium»
A place for cutting truth: a body of work where material brought in is dissected until each piece can contribute on its own, adjudicated and refined toward agreement with the truth it is held to, and reassembled into coherent results for others to comprehend and build on; worked on under high turnover; and true as stated, with its conditions, whenever stationary.

## Definition
A verisectorium takes in **influx**, material from outside its settled content, and **cuts** it until the pieces are independently contributory: each able to be right or wrong, decided or open, on its own. The pieces are **adjudicated** by minds and instruments whose acts are recorded; what is adjudicated is always one of two kinds of statement, a truth claim, which stands by evidence, or a «decision», which stands by the backing of its party. They are **refined** toward agreement with what the work is held to. And they are **reassembled** into coherent results that readers and downstream builders use and build on.

Concretely a verisectorium is: an **ontology** (the kinds of thing it may hold, the «dimensions|def/dimension» asked of them, and which of those are ‹determinant›s); a **corpus** of everything written in it; its **acta** of everything done to it; a **history**, the changesets that have closed and the «steady·states|def/steady-state» they reached; and its **praxes**, what is expected of a mind working in it that no tool enforces. The word also names this repository and project, which describes verisectoria generally and builds the tooling (`v2`) for them; that sense is kept because the estate uses it, and it is not the referent of the relations above.

### Machinery and practical model
Two things are defined in this outline and must not be confused. The **machinery** is the meta-ontology the rest of the outline gives: its terms, under which content divides without end in principle and stops in practice at a few scales: the «veritoid», the document-like «segments|def/segment» veritoids are written in, and the projections that gather those. The **practical model** is what a mind at work actually meets: an outline with marked slots, document-like segments rendered as markdown, an inbox beside each segment, a frontier to read, a steady state to reach, and the affordances each of those wants. The practical model is built on the machinery and is not the same thing as it; a tool may give an outline affordances a bare projection does not name without the machinery changing. Where the outline speaks of both, it says which. What the practical model cannot embed deterministically is written for the mind as a **praxis**: the engine enforces what it can, and the praxes say what is expected beyond that.

### Invariants
- Its ontology declares at least one ‹determinant›.
- Its ontology declares how a segment enters canon: by whose decision, or by what rule over which warrants, or by the act of placing it unmarked in the canonical projection. Until it does, nothing is in canon, nothing owes, and no frontier can be computed.
- At least one of its projections is canonical, however experimental or nascent, and it is true as stated (for example, as initial untested hypotheses) at every steady state.
- A "provisional" segment of any kind is, to canon, only a claim that the suggestion for such a segment (etc.) exists.

## Discussion
- **Why influx comes first in the definition.** Every verisectorium in the estate began the same way and keeps beginning that way: a pile of quotes pulled on a subject, spikes and generative drafts, empirical results, audits, an earlier theory's files, the notes a project was worked out in. The dissection is the thing itself, not a preliminary to it, and under turnover it is what lets the work compound instead of being redone: new results are built on adjudicated ones. In a theory the branching chain of claims is the engine of discovery; in a specification the accumulating decisions are.
- **Truth-centric.** What a verisectorium is for is agreement with the dimensions it declares as determinants: validity against logic, agreement with the world, fidelity to a source, consistency within a canon, and so on. Which, and against what, is each verisectorium's declaration, and the first invariant requires at least one, because a verisectorium with no determinant has no sense of better. What it claims as better as a whole is made from its determinants, weighted by the purpose that reads the pieces; how is argued in `disc-better`. The *veri* in the name is broad on purpose: for an explanation or a tool, being understood or working as intended is what being right comes to.
- **Continually improving.** It is rarely finished. Over time it is expected to get better: answering better to its ‹determinant› «dimensions|def/dimension», or shown more firmly to. Better is a direction and not a score or a queue, and where effort goes next is a decision. Refining is the daily work: strengthen, re-derive, narrow, decide. The honesty machinery exists so that the result of that work stays trustworthy while it is done.
- **Turnover-resilient.** Many minds who do not know it beforehand must be able to work on it, with limited time and attention, and leave it better and not noisier. This is why warrants are read from acts and never typed, why the frontier is computed and never kept, and why the inbox is outside what a segment says. It is also why only a «changeset» writes and nothing is changed by hand: a change that did not go through a changeset left no actum, and a verisectorium whose corpus and acta can disagree has no steady state anyone can trust.
- **Self-consistency is intrinsic.** Steady-state stability as a self-consistency check is the one motivation every verisectorium shares; the constraints the «frontier» lists as its items (nothing in canon asserting what binding evidence contradicts, or said to stand better than it does; every decision canon rests on still standing; references holding; promises kept; questions answered) are what the model and tools supply to all of them without declaration. What exactly holds when a verisectorium is stationary is the «steady·state» def's to state; the one-line definition here says only that it does.
- **Why one projection is always canonical, and why provisional claims so little.** A mind arriving cold needs one answer to "what is the work, in what order," or it either adheres too closely to whatever draft outline it finds or stops telling grounded from ungrounded and treats the whole as an artifact task. A canonical projection full of empty slots can still be that answer, because a suggested slot, or a provisional segment of any kind, claims only that the suggestion exists; that is what keeps the projection true as stated at every steady state while most of the work is unwritten. The states of a slot, and what accepting a segment is, are the «segment» def's.
- **Content and notes.** Content no dimension can be asked of cannot be adjudicated, owed or warranted, and the corpus is for what can be; such material is a note about the verisectorium and lives in an inbox or outside. Whether every kind must therefore open a dimension is the «kind» def's invariant, and a choice.
- **The engine's gaps.** Where the engine cannot express a change a mind needs to make, that is how the engine's gap is found: the change is recorded as a demand on the engine, not made by hand around it.

## Examples
- AAT: began as earlier theory files and sprint audits brought in as influx; claims answering to logic under their stated hypotheses; results built on results; delivered as volumes, a front door, papers and pedagogy. Better is stronger claims more firmly established.
- A formal specification (udon): began as usage, demands and discussion; rules answering to their own consistency and to the intent behind them; decisions accumulating with grounds and a path to revisit; delivered as the spec tooling is built from.
- A compiled product (comproprium, ai-risk-model, relata): began as sources; pieces answering to the works they came from, each named by a reference; delivered as a system prompt, a response store, a bibliography that other work consumes.
- A novel's world: facts answering to the world's own canon and to the author's intent; better is self-consistency and fidelity.
- This repository's `core/`: began as the working notes and transcripts of its own design, cut and read; a model answering to coherence read whole, consistency among its terms, and whether a cold reader can build an instance from it; delivered as what `v2` and each verisectorium's ontology are built from.

## Historical Context
### Etymology
*Verisectorium*: a place (*-orium*, as in auditorium, sanatorium) for cutting (*secare*, whence section, dissect) truth (*veritas*). Coined by Joseph before any of this model existed, for the estate's living bodies of work, and the literal image, a dissection table for truth, turned out to be the operational definition: influx is cut until its pieces can stand alone. Nothing ancient that we know of names the thing; *corpus* names only what it holds.

### The two atoms
- Joseph, 2026-11-10, naming the reduction he had reached in the earlier notes and dialogs: "I came to see "truth related dimensions"/adjudication and (the truth about) "decision related dimensions"/adjudication/routing/etc. (based on attestation and standing rather than epistemology) as the two critical atomic components that everything else ends up reducing to... even attributes reduce to the machine making an attestation of a truth claim and automated actions are decisions...  It seems maybe that part of the mathematical spine may have been lost..."
- Where it stood: `msc/notes/02-2026-11-04-plan.md` §A0 names four primitives (truth claim; decision, whose standing is its backing; attested fact, "the floor where recursion stops"; attribute) and says "Everything else is composed of these", with "a rule is a decision whose application is computed". His sort of 2026-11-04 by "what builds/founds/sustains this axis" (quoted in `msc/notes/18`, "Joseph's own words") gives the two their different foundations: certainty "changed with and requires (accumulated) evidence"; decision "decided at an inflection/branch point with certain basis-- requires accountable parties".
- His own statements of it, from his messages of the days the plan was written (`~/.claude/history.jsonl`, read whole for the twenty-five messages that speak of decision together with truth, standing or attestation). 2026-11-04 19:54: "Does the plan do a good job of building from the primitives? Through the first core and the first days of this one the pair survived only as two sorts of act; it was rebuilt into the spine, «decision», «dimension» and «act» on 2026-11-10.

## Working Notes
- **What is canon: answered by Joseph, 2026-11-10**, after the second real root could not compute a frontier for asf without it. "The old idea of "canon is whatever the verisectorium says it is-- usually the main outline's non-provisional slots" still sounds like the right move generally speaking. Sometimes what will happen is if a verisectorium gets so tied in knots and needs a fresh approach altogether and it's decided that a new corpus and fixed ontology are the way to go-- the existing "canon" is just marked "not canon anymore--> influx to new corpus" and they get a whole fresh start. That's exactly what we intend to do with AAT.  Other times more than one isolated subgraph will be considered canon in terms of steady-state and self-made obligations of truth--  simply mark the two outlines (assembly segments) that are the two "canon" roots. In practice, it often boils down to the projections and purpose of various outlines and therefore what the most important determinants are for that purpose/audience". So an asf segment in a non-provisional slot of the main outline is canon whatever its stage field says. What this asks of the defs, not yet done: canon is reached from marked roots, there may be several, and what a piece is held to is what the roots reaching it ask.
