## Intro

A «BOUND» is a declared limit on a «veritoid», consulted at a moment and never earned by degrees: what the piece may assert on its own authority, what a reader may do with it, whether it is safe to show an unprimed auditor, which of two conflicting things yields.

## Definition

A declared limit on a veritoid, consulted at a moment and never earned by degrees.
- It is ‹written›, and it is not an answer to any «dimension».
- It is consulted, not accumulated. There are no degrees of a bound.

## Discussion

- Bounds are what separate, in an implications segment, the sentences that restate proved results from the ones that are "this segment's own framing": the segment's bound says it may restate and frame but may not assert anything new, and the marked sentences are the ones that step past it.

## Examples

- What this piece may assert on its own authority: nothing new; a summary; a generalization; an original claim.
- What a reader may do with it: usable as a hypothesis; do not revive.
- Whether it is safe to show an unprimed auditor.
- Which of two conflicting things yields.
